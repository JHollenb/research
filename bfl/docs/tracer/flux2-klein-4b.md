# FLUX.2 Klein 4B

The distilled Klein 4B checkpoint has 5 joint blocks, 20 single-stream blocks, 24 heads, and 625 tracer components. Its role budget is 65.75% operation/MLP, 7.31% each for the four attention-facing address/selector/payload/carrier families, 4.40% clock, and 0.62% content.

The bounded live probe reconstructed 25 layers and 600 heads. `D0MLP` was the strongest tested lesion at `0.165068` internal MSE; `D0H19`, `D0H7`, and other first-joint heads led local sensitivity. Separate matched-seed prompt/image work found a distributed lexical-to-spatial transport trend through early joint sites, redundant image-token support, the scheduler return register, and RGB output. It remains a bounded trend: no minimal, sufficient, or universal circuit was established.

This is the primary checkpoint for the BFL serving, route, hotpatch, and recipient-facing demos. Those consumer-facing results must not be generalized to another checkpoint without fresh capture, intervention, parity, and native-consumer evidence.
