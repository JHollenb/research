The new [bridge paper](../../scaffolds-and-dynamic-circuits/bridge-paper.md) asks which functional responsibilities recur while prompts, queries, and carried history instantiate different causal support. ## Changes accepted

| Review concern | Revision |

FLUX's exhaustive singleton/whole-path matrix identifies strict support distribution; timing, continuation, and carried-register evidence add history information. The matrix alone is not called a mapped source-to-store mediator. |
| “Endogenous” could imply donor-free construction | Both papers say recipient-formed **after a supplied clean residual**. The rescue recipient receives already formed state; its native suffix computes its answer. Host-supplied source addresses and donor assistance remain explicit. |
| E20 could imply the complete formation trajectory was mapped | The paper identifies a formation interval and causal endpoint; intermediate carriers, writers, and edges remain unresolved. |
| Commitment could mean only cache persistence | Commitment is acquisition of independent causal authority at a declared runtime interface. “Hardening” means persistent influence with later rewriting, redundancy, or repair possible. Training-time learning and inference-time formation are separate clocks. |
| A subtype certificate could become the entire contribution | Qwen is the primary bounded causal-mechanism explanation. FLUX is the threshold-certified strict path-distributed instance at the declared cuts. Neither result grants a universal architecture claim. |
| Same stored patch/different question could be used to reject static storage | The bridge claims query-conditioned consumption and retains static stored content with dynamic attention, conventional lookup, contextual copying, and redundancy as possible implementations. |

Reuse across executions is distinct from carried-state feedback. |

## What remains a research question

The two papers do not claim a complete internal construction graph, donor-free semantic compiler, automatic query-to-source addressing, universal symbol table, isolated eye module, autonomous semantic feedback loop, or a portable cross-family tensor circuit. The scaffold hypothesis calls for prospective frozen role profiles and comparisons against architecture, depth, and energy explanations. These are next evidence cuts, not retrospective reasons to discard the existing trends.

The durable explanatory point is already supported in the controlled records: a local source can initiate formation of later authoritative state; that state's necessity, direct write ports, and read can have different support profiles. The bridge then makes reuse testable by holding stored state and intervention bytes fixed while changing the native operation that consumes them.
