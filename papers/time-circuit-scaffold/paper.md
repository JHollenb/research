# Dynamic Circuits Loop Through a Stable Time Scaffold

Jacob Hollenbeck

*Submission manuscript, 2026-10-05. Companion to [Time-Formed Circuits in Transformers](../time-formed-circuits/paper.md), which establishes the circuit class this paper reuses and carries the cross-family causal-band table (its Table 2 / Figure 7).*

## Abstract

A trained language model, read for factual recall, factors into a small store that later computation reads by address and an input-dependent routing program computed from carried state. We report that factorization findings-first. (1) A byte-identical stored key/value state takes different causal authority when only the question changes: patching the queried fact rather than another fact adds a median 3.130 nats of harm to the correct answer for color (16 of 16 contrasts) and 0.583 nats for position (16 of 16). (2) A 24-coordinate reader profile frozen before evaluation predicts held spatial-relation effects across all eight held contexts, beating three frozen competitors (mapped cosine 0.954 against an operation-blind 0.566), and permuting the two trained value projections collapses it (relation 0.955 to 0.049). (3) Fine-tuning conserves where the store is read while rewriting how it is routed: the late read band is conserved across base, instruct, coder, and math variants, and the band code transfers base-to-derivative (base-to-coder 0.92) but not derivative-to-base (coder-to-base 0.34, math-to-base 0.05). (4) A fit-free locator that reads the model rather than fitting depth predicts the commit band blind in two new families (Phi-2 6 of 6, OLMo-2 5 of 6), but its band is salience, not causality: on four deeper models it hits the causal commit within two layers only half the time, and a necessary late band must be found by causal search, not attention salience. (5) One band frozen before data ties a strong per-input rival across three operation classes (fixed-over-ceiling 0.85 to 1.00). (6) The cross-family common role graph is a null, and a function-vector account of the reader fails.

The headline sentence is a framing that no deterministic stateful network can violate; the falsifiable core is the store/router factorization, and we score it. Of eight commitments, six hold for this task family (query-gated participation, formed-committed-read, address and payload separation, history-formed state, prospective reader, learned organization), one fired in the present round (blind findability in deep nets), and two are narrowed by two further firings in the same round: a band-only key/value cache for old context retrieves at 0.000, and borrowed "routing" smuggles a donor's answer through its MLP gates. The surviving statement is: a late commit band exists per model for this task family, its location is architecture-dependent, and it must be found causally rather than by attention salience. Evidence is one laboratory, one harness, mostly single seed, at and below 7B.

*Scope and vocabulary. The named hypothesis is the **time scaffold**: a reusable organization of causal roles (source, address/reader, writer, carried state, consumer) laid out along ordered execution, identified by intervening on recurring roles rather than by a layer index. It is defined once here and used in standard interpretability terms throughout. Experiment identifiers, harness names, and job IDs are confined to Appendix C.*

## 1. Findings

Every quantity is measured through the model's unchanged native consumer — full-vocabulary logits or native greedy continuation for language models, the native scheduler and decoder for diffusion. Effective sample sizes are stated per claim; most language-model results are single seed under a prototype-first policy. Each number is labelled MEASURED, with its source file given per finding in Appendix C; judgments are labelled ASSERTED.

### 1.1 A byte-identical stored state has query-dependent authority

The cleanest result holds the stored object fixed and changes only the question. In Qwen2.5-1.5B we fix a late key/value store and its intervention bytes exactly — same parent state, same layer band, same positions, same tensor digests — and change only the native question (MEASURED).

Asking for the patched fact rather than another fact increases the patch's harm to the correct answer by a median 3.130 nats for color, positive in all 16 of 16 contrasts; by 0.583 nats for position, positive in all 16 of 16; and by 3.021 nats for a position-to-color lookup, positive in all 16 of 16, with 128 directional pairing checks passing (MEASURED, same file). The authority of the same stored object is selected by the operation, not fixed to the object. A query-dependent lookup is a compatible implementation; the result establishes selective consumption of a fixed interface, not its internal algorithm (ASSERTED).

### 1.2 A reader profile frozen before evaluation predicts held relation contexts

A 24-coordinate signed reader profile and three competitors — an operation-blind mean, a wrong-operation profile, and a within-band coordinate permutation — were frozen before any held context was opened, then evaluated on two lexical sets crossed with two templates and two orders (MEASURED).

For relation the frozen profile beats every competitor in raw error in all eight held contexts: mapped cosine 0.954 against the operation-blind 0.566, raw RMSE 0.029 against 0.149 (MEASURED, same file). The prediction is operation-specific: color retains the broad shape but misses its reader ordering in five of eight, identity does not separate from the operation-blind profile, and size is unavailable under the held format (MEASURED, same file; eight correlated contexts, single seed).

The profile depends on specific trained value projections. Permuting all 256 rows of the two layer-22 and layer-23 value projections changes the mapped cosine from 0.955 to 0.049 for relation, 0.838 to 0.110 for color, and 0.931 to 0.569 for identity, while native competence also falls (MEASURED, same file). This is a weight-dependence result, not a behavior-matched null, because the permuted network is less competent (ASSERTED).

### 1.3 Fine-tuning conserves the read location and rewrites the routed code, asymmetrically

A single lineage (Qwen2.5-1.5B base, instruct, coder, math, and the two domain-instruct variants) separates the scaffold from the program (MEASURED).

The read location is conserved. The fit-free locator curve is bimodal with lobes at layers 21 to 23 and layer 27 in all six variants, and five of six peak at layer 27; the band is where even the most rewired variant is most similar to the base (math centered-kernel alignment 0.72 in band against 0.41 mid-depth) (MEASURED, same file). Domain training is not a small nudge sparing the band: coder weights sit at cosine 0.47 to 0.72 to base and math at 0.20 to 0.42, spread evenly across depth, while instruct tuning is a uniform roughly 1.2% change (MEASURED, same file).

The band code is shared but directional. Transplanting the formed band key/value transfers base-to-coder at 0.92 and base-to-math at 0.43, but coder-to-base at only 0.34 and math-to-base at only 0.05, against a base self-transplant bound of 0.63; instruct is symmetric with base (about 0.51 and 0.57) (MEASURED, same file). The domain models read the base's band code, but write a drifted code the base decodes progressively worse (ASSERTED). This is the first falsifier-edge of commitment C7 (Section 2): location survives training; the code does not transfer symmetrically.

### 1.4 The blind locator: real hits, and misses that relocate the band causally

Predicting the location of the commit band is harder than predicting a reader profile, and a rule fit to depth alone fails it. A depth-fraction band (layers [round(0.75n), round(0.96n))) missed entirely in Pythia-1.4B, where the source-to-store dissociation instead sat at a shallower band near layer 16 (MEASURED).

A fit-free locator recovers the band where the depth rule cannot, because it reads the model. It scores each layer by the attention-weighted value contribution from the final position to the subject position, with the sink column excluded, and takes a band around its peak; the statistic and band rule were hash-frozen before any score was computed (MEASURED). Retrospectively on four known cells it beats the depth rule on mean band overlap, 0.615 against 0.490, and in Pythia-1.4B its peak lands on layer 16, the true commit, where the depth rule scores 0 (MEASURED, same file). Blind on two further families it passes six of six interventional checks on Phi-2 (peak layer 23 is the true commit; its band carries the mediation at block 0.515 against the depth band's 0.065) and five of six on OLMo-2-1B, where the one failure is that the commit is nearly a single layer on a 16-layer model rather than distributed (MEASURED, same file; two blind models, base models only).

The locator predicts salience, not necessity, and that gap shows in deeper nets. Across seven models including four scored blind, the locator hits the causal commit band within two layers only half the time (within-2 fraction 0.5), with mean band overlap 0.565 against a depth rule's 0.402 and a random band's 0.260 (MEASURED). Running the full source-to-store panel with the band chosen blind by the locator on four non-Qwen families, transplant sufficiency reproduces in all four (rescue 0.64 to 1.62, every confidence interval excluding 0), but block necessity holds in only two: OLMo-2-1B (1.09) and Pythia-1.4B (1.29) pass, while Phi-2 (0.03) and GPT-2 XL (0.16) fail, and for GPT-2 XL a matched-width earlier band removes more (0.364) than the locator's band (MEASURED).

A causal search then shows the two deep-model failures were locator misses, not an absent necessary band. A joint contiguous-window necessity sweep (validated as a method canary, since it recovers the locator band exactly where the locator already worked: OLMo-2-1B overlap 1.00 at block 1.087, Pythia-1.4B overlap 0.71 at block 1.44) locates GPT-2 XL's causal commit at layers 31 to 42, where the band block removes 0.888 of the seed gain against 0.160 at the blind salience band (layers 36 to 47) and collapses the correct candidate from 1.00 to 0.19; Phi-2's causal band is layers 21 to 28 at block 0.485, roughly fifteen times the 0.033 at its blind band, though no width-8 window reaches the 0.6 threshold, so Phi-2 is partly distributed (MEASURED). The band is selected by the same rows it is scored on; a split-halves check returns the identical window in 197 of 200 random splits for GPT-2 XL and 200 of 200 for Phi-2, with identical held-out medians (MEASURED, same file). These rows are folded into the companion paper's Table 2 and Figure 7; we cite them there rather than duplicate the table.

The reading is that a necessary late commit band exists per model (seven of eight decoder cells, Phi-2 partly distributed), its location is architecture-dependent, and the blind attention-salience locator is a cheap but fallible finder that must be backed by causal search in deep nets (ASSERTED).

### 1.5 One band frozen before data ties a strong per-input rival across operations

A single band frozen at layers 22 to 27 before any data, at the subject key/value position, was tested on three operation classes against a non-trivial per-input oracle that searches 232 to 288 sites per input (bands of width 2 to 8, per-head subsets, operand, operator, and intermediate positions), excluding the layer-0 token-identity site (MEASURED).

The fixed band reaches 0.852 of the oracle ceiling on arithmetic, 0.950 on recall, and 1.000 on two-hop, and the oracle's best site contains the band entry layer in 100% of pairs in every class (MEASURED, same file). The computed-arithmetic commit is two-positioned: a single-position rescue is a borderline 0.485, but the joint subject-and-second-operand band rescue is 0.925, as preregistered (MEASURED, same file). The recall-band locator miss reproduces (layer 27 against the causal layer 22), consistent with Section 1.4 (MEASURED, same file; single seed, one model).

### 1.6 The cross-family common graph is a null, and a function-vector account fails

A connected cross-family forecast of a held effect from the role graph gives a mean absolute error of 0.015099 logit units against 0.014593 for an operation-blind constant, ties a four-feature additive baseline to the digit at 0.015099, and wins only one of three held contexts; a common role-matcher costs 6.9125 against a depth null's 6.9511, a gap of 0.0386 (MEASURED). On these tests a shared cross-family graph cannot be told apart from addition or matched depth (ASSERTED). We report this as its own result, not a headline (Figure 4).

![Figure 4. The cross-family common-graph claim is a null. A connected forecast ties a plain additive baseline exactly (MAE 0.015099 vs 0.014593 operation-blind) and the best role-matcher costs 6.9125 against a depth null's 6.9511, a gap of 0.0386. The within-model scaffold does not need a shared cross-family graph.](figures/fig7_honest_null.png)

The null network control is learned but bounded. On an untrained and a weight-shuffled Qwen2.5-1.5B of identical shape, the coordinate main effect that organizes the reader is 0.012 and 0.014 against 0.381 in the trained network, so the structure is learned; but both nulls have native top-1 accuracy of 0 of 96, so they are behaviorally dead, and the discriminating control (a competent non-scaffold network) is not run (MEASURED). We disclose a failed prediction in the same run: the frozen relational reader beats the operation-blind profile in the random-initialized network too, by 0.350, which weakens the learned reading for that one operation (MEASURED, same file).

A function-vector account of the reader fails, and we state it as a failure. An operation function vector (relation minus identity output-projection means) injected at layer 24 produces no top-1 flips (0 of 14) and a positive target-logprob change of +2.19 nats, and does not rebuild the relation route; the verdict is NOT a function vector (MEASURED, same file). The caveats are explicit: the coefficient was fixed at 1.0 and not swept, the vector sums over all heads rather than top-attribution heads, the injection layer sat at the top of the searched grid, and the vector was extracted from all contexts including the held ones (MEASURED, same file). A stronger follow-up with swept scale and top-attribution heads has not been run (ASSERTED).

## 2. The architecture hypothesis and its falsifiers

The hypothesis is that the computation controlling a model's output is organized as a reusable set of causal roles — a contextual source, a live reader or transition, a writer, carried state, and a native consumer — laid out along ordered execution, such that the machinery recurs across inputs while the per-input dynamic circuit (which source, which address, which payload, which dose, which timing) loops through it, reading carried state, writing new state, and thereby shaping the next operation. One step of the loop is an address `a_t = A(s, u_t, z_t)`, a write `w_t = W(s, u_t, z_t, a_t)`, a transition `z_{t+1} = T(z_t, w_t)`, and a consumer read `y_t = C(z_{t+1}, u_t)`; "static" means a fixed role lowering, not a fixed layer index.

That sentence is a framing, not a finding: any deterministic stateful network satisfies it, so it cannot fail on its own. The falsifiable core is a store/router factorization — a trained model exposes one fixed, findable interface where content is committed and later read, shared across operations, surviving continued training, sufficient as partial state, with per-input computation routed over it. Eight commitments spell this out and each names its breaking outcome. They are labelled C1 through C8. Every "holds" is a retrodiction except where marked prospective; the blind locator (C2) and the cross-operation band (C1) are the only tests frozen before data.

| C2 | The scaffold can be located from native reads alone | A fit-free locator, hash-frozen before the job, misses the commit band in an unseen model | **fired** for deep nets: blind locator finds salience, not the causal band, on Phi-2 and GPT-2 XL; it holds only where salience coincides with the commit (§1.4) |
| C3 | Content forms over execution, is committed at a band, and is read there | Blocking the store leaves the effect, rescue fails, or the read is not at the stored position | **held**, one task family: six families plus 7B, block 0.924/rescue 0.871; extended to parametric attribute, relation, two-hop content in two families |

| C5 | Address and payload are separate (K addresses, V carries the value) | A value-only swap fails to move content while a key-only swap does, or the alias residual carries the value | **held** across two architectures, largely as attention algebra predicts: value-only 0.89–0.93, key-only 0.0; key-by-value interaction +0.34 in SDXL |

### 2.1 The three falsifier-firings of the present round, and what each removes

**Blind findability in deep nets (C2).** With the commit band chosen blind by the attention-salience locator, the source-to-store block necessity reproduces in the two shallow families but fails on Phi-2 (0.03) and GPT-2 XL (0.16), where the causal commit sits earlier than the salience peak (§1.4). This removes "the machine can be found blind from one forward pass" for deep networks. A causal joint-window search still finds a necessary band (GPT-2 XL 0.888, Phi-2 0.485 partly distributed; §1.4), so what is removed is blind salience-based findability, not the existence of the band.

**Band-only key/value memory retrieves at 0.000 (engineering extrapolation of C3/C5).** Keeping old context (512 to 8192 tokens back) visible only in the commit band's six layers destroys in-context recall and binding: accuracy falls to 0.000 on Qwen2.5-1.5B base and instruct, the same as a window-only floor, while evenly spaced and random six-layer sets also fail and a band-centered twelve-layer set recovers 0.34 to 0.46 (MEASURED). This removes the idea that the band is a self-sufficient memory that can be paged alone: storage is banded, but addressing is not — early-to-mid layers must read the past to build the query that the band then commits and reads. It does not refute C3, whose transplants leave all other context intact (ASSERTED).

**Borrowed routing smuggles content through MLP gates (narrows the "router" half).** Running a recipient's payloads with a same-operation donor's full frozen routing (attention weights, MLP gate activations, normalization scales) makes the model output the donor's answer in all three operation classes (recipient-correct 0.00 to 0.03, donor-answer 0.97 to 1.00); the MLP gate activations alone reproduce this, while attention weights alone or norms alone execute the recipient correctly (MEASURED). This removes "per-input computation is routing over a shared store" in its simple form: the MLP gate activations are content, not routing, so the only exactly affine regime (everything frozen) is the content-smuggling one and cannot be reused as a compiled circuit. Attention patterns remain input-agnostic routing within a template (ASSERTED).

The surviving statement is narrow and sharp: **a late commit band exists per model for this task family, its location is architecture-dependent, and it must be found causally rather than by attention salience.** Six commitments hold for in-context and parametric entity-attribute readout up to 7B; the cross-family common role graph is a measured null (§1.6, Figure 4), and the claim is untested on the operation classes that would make it an architecture theory rather than a better-instrumented extraction account.

## 3. Related measured results from the same runtime

The store/router factorization predicts a division of labor: static infrastructure (the committed store, the carried-state planes) versus dynamic routing and dispatch. Three results from the same laboratory bear on that division. Each is a single cell — one organism, checkpoint, and seed unless stated — and is framed as such; cell sizes are quoted from the linked primary records, not from any summary.

**Conflicting capabilities need a recipient-native dispatch gate, not a merge (dynamic routing).** Two Pythia-70m specialist operations wrote additive updates to the same layer-3 boundary. Their gradients had cosine −0.6303, and 73.19% of jointly nonzero coordinates had opposing signs; every static linear, TIES, and orthogonal merge sacrificed at least one capability, and the best choice was a no-op with zero retained gain (MEASURED). Preserving both specialists and adding a 513-parameter affine dispatch gate over the recipient's own layer-3 state recovered both on frozen held data: capability A from 43.75% to 100%, capability B from 52.08% to 93.75%, with the gate classifying all 96 held routes correctly, and a fresh donor-free process reproducing the result (MEASURED, same file). This is one synthetic binary conflict, one checkpoint, one seed — a single cell. It shows that a failed merge can be an architectural diagnosis: preserve the programs and route between them (ASSERTED).

**Useful capability lives outside the donor's geometry (dynamic routing geometry).** A correction trained through the recipient's own consumer was decomposed against a 26-dimensional donor-target subspace built from 128 discovery rows: 87.50% of the useful held-out correction energy lay orthogonal to the donor subspace (86.48% on discovery) (MEASURED). At one dose, a trace imitation solved 47 of 128 held rows, the donor-parallel component 53, the recipient-orthogonal component 74, and the full correction 77, with a wrong-source control at 1 of 128 (MEASURED, same file; one task, one checkpoint pair, one adapter seed — a single cell). A companion cross-family result sharpens the lesson that native-consumer equivalence, not representational similarity, is the contract: swapping complete typed conditioner returns from SmolLM2 and Mamba for a Qwen donor's returns into a shared frozen diffusion decoder cut the image distance to the Qwen render from 82.3186 to 23.4780 (71.5%) and from 83.1801 to 23.9645 (71.2%), while a multi-seed controller produced positive targetward progress across three seeds (0.6471 and 0.6694 mean progress) (MEASURED). A held-out carrier cosine of 0.6602 with weak consumer repair in the one traceable carrier-cosine record (MEASURED) supports the same lesson: representational similarity of the carrier does not certify the behavior (ASSERTED).

**The key/value cache is a position clock plus an address plane plus a causal residual (static infrastructure).** Over a 2,048-token natural-text stream in two small Qwen models, subtracting the analytic rotary position clock raised within-token phase coherence from 0.512/0.544 to 0.870/0.864 (earliest layers 0.98 to 1.00), so at shallow depth a key is its token's direction on the position clock (MEASURED). The part of the write not explained by the per-token codebook — the contextual residual — is 0.19 to 0.22 relative L2, and it is load-bearing: continuing generation from a codebook-only cache agrees with the source on just 3 to 8% of tokens (MEASURED, same file; one organism, a single cell). The cache is therefore neither opaque memory nor a token dictionary; it has an analytic clock, a readable address plane, and a causally consumed residual — the static interface the store/router factorization requires (ASSERTED).

## 4. Prior art, with the delta

The nearest predecessor to the scaffold is the staged, depth-structured view of inference. Geva et al. separate subject enrichment, relation propagation, and extraction into stages ([Geva et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14767)); our non-lexical-store result that content is MLP-built at the subject and committed at a late band is consistent with that enrichment-then-extraction picture. The delta is causal: we identify roles by signed intervention, transplant the formed band into an unseeded recipient, and show the same interface hosts several operations, rather than inferring stages from aggregate statistics.

The factual-recall localization and editing line is the most direct comparison. Meng et al. locate factual associations in mid-layer MLPs and edit them, and Hase et al. show localization and editing can come apart ([Meng et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05262); [Hase et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04213)). Contemporaneous work studies when and where recall commits and how entities bind: Agarwal reports relation information becoming generation-controlling before entity information ([Agarwal 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17537)); Cheng and Zhang ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04061)) and Hochman, Shapira, and Goldberg ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21345)) bear on the depth and the binding structure of recall. Our delta is to measure where the controlling state commits for read-out, to show it is a portable object (a band formed by a seeded recipient authors the answer when transplanted into a recipient that never saw the seed), and to show its authority is query-gated for a byte-identical patch (§1.1).

Context-dependent binding and reusable operation carriers are the closest predecessors to the dynamic-circuit side. Feng and Steinhardt identify binding-identity representations through causal interventions ([Feng and Steinhardt 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17191)); Todd et al. and Hendel et al. carry a reusable operation as a compact vector ([Todd et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15213); [Hendel et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15916)). Our own function-vector equivalence test (§1.6) is the direct comparison: a single relation-minus-identity output-projection vector does not reproduce the signed reader (0 of 14 flips, +2.19 nats), so the reader is not that additive direction at unit scale — stated with its caveats, not as a refutation of function vectors in general.

The model-merging line motivates the dispatch result. TIES-Merging resolves sign conflicts when combining fine-tuned models, and Branch-Train-MiX trains experts separately and mixes them ([TIES, Yadav et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01708); [Branch-Train-MiX, Sukhbaatar et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07816)). Our Section 3 dispatch result is a controlled case where linear, TIES, and orthogonal merges all fail on a genuine same-boundary sign conflict (cosine −0.6303) and a small recipient-native gate recovers both capabilities — evidence for dispatch over merging, on one synthetic conflict.

The key/value-compression line is the right reference for the band-only negative. StreamingLLM keeps attention sinks plus a window, PyramidKV keeps more tokens in lower layers, and cross-layer / layer-condensed key/value methods share the cache across layers through training. Our band-only result (§2.1) is consistent with all of them: a post-hoc band-only cache retrieves at 0.000, so banded key/value memory must be trained in, not imposed after the fact.

Finally, attribution-graph and automated-circuit methods are the tools the scaffold is partly invisible to; the companion paper shows one such method placing most subject influence at the first layer and seeing at most 0.08 of the effect in the commit band while a native store cut flips the answer 83% of the time. Those tools find the per-input dynamic circuit; the scaffold is in the state they treat as an endpoint.

## 5. Limitations, and what an outside replication needs

The breadth is real but narrow. The role graph is measured most completely in Qwen2.5-1.5B and Gemma-2-2B; the source-to-store edge reproduces across six-plus transformer families and at 7B, but the cross-family common graph is a null (§1.6) and there is no positive result above 7B. Effective sample sizes are small and correlated, the replication unit is the context rather than the intervention, and most language-model results are single seed under a prototype-first policy. The blind location prediction rests on a thin sample: two blind models for the original locator task and four for the causal-band follow-up, base models only, with a Gemma locator miss of about three layers.

One instrument defect bears on how much of this work to trust. A retired feedback-and-selector instrument installed a custom attention function without its matching causal mask, so a multi-token query chunk could attend to future tokens — a correctness failure that nonetheless passed the instrument's own replay checks. The affected routing measurement was re-run under stock eager execution with zero future attention in every preceding query row, and it reproduced the qualitative effect; the headline findings in this paper all run on stock Hugging Face eager execution. The lesson is that a verification apparatus certifies that an instrument ran, not that its result is valid, so the headline numbers should be re-derived by independent groups.

**What an outside replication needs**, all available in the public release:

- **Checkpoints.** Qwen2.5-1.5B (base and instruct) and Gemma-2-2B for the reader and store results; SmolLM2-1.7B, OLMo-2-0425-1B, Pythia-1.4B, Phi-2, and GPT-2 XL for the cross-family and blind-locator panels; Qwen2.5-7B (paged, float32) for scale; Mamba-130M/370M/2.8B and FLUX/SDXL for the other families.

## Appendix A. Methods

**Native consumer.** Every language-model claim is read from the unchanged full-vocabulary logits or native greedy continuation; every diffusion claim from the native scheduler, latent bridge, and decoder. No probe or replacement head is substituted for the consumer.

**Numerics.** Prospective runs use stock Hugging Face eager attention, CUDA, float32, tf32 off, seed 0, model loaded once, branches run in process. The 7B specimen is executed one decoder layer at a time to fit a 16 GB card. Diffusion runs use the native pipeline precision and report RGB equality by decoded-array and hash.

**Admission.** A context enters a quantitative panel only if the clean model answers it correctly with a clean-minus-filler log-probability of at least 1.0. Non-admitted contexts are retained and excluded as native-incompetent; the admitted count is the effective n.

**Gates.** Each prospective cell passes an isolated no-op, a residual-write no-op, and a cache-path parity check, or the cell is void. Store necessity is an isolated whole-band or single-layer key/value swap at the subject position; the formation window is a single residual write of the clean subject state.

## Appendix B. Reproducibility and frozen designs

The reader-profile prediction froze a 24-coordinate profile and three competitors before any held context was opened. The blind locator froze its statistic, three candidate band rules, and the single selection criterion with source hashes before any score was computed, and wrote each model's predicted band to a hash-frozen file one to two minutes before the scoring job was created. The cross-operation band and the arithmetic joint-rescue prediction are in the break-it preregistration. The deep-model causal-band sweep added a joint-window amendment, documented before the scored jobs, and reports a split-halves selection check (197/200 and 200/200). Several reader-profile freezes rest on a file-timestamp record rather than a hash; this is stated where it applies. Because the toolchain has shown a silent correctness defect in a retired instrument, the headline claims should be re-derived by independent groups on stock execution.
