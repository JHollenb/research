# Dynamic circuits: availability, recruitment, and counting

Status: working interpretation and an exploratory next cut; no terminal census claim.

The existing evidence supports reusable causal interfaces with context-dependent content, addresses, and live readers. It does not identify a fixed inventory of independently stored semantic operators. Keep three timelines separate: acquisition across training checkpoints, formation through depth within a forward pass, and recruitment as conversation state grows.

## What can exist before what?

At fixed weights, learned transformations are available before a new prompt, while their effective composition and intermediate state depend on that prompt. Within a forward pass, the [formation-over-time results](../paper.md) sharpen this distinction: content can arrive already formed at a late commit/read band. Upstream residual-seed continuation and late-store consumption therefore concern different roles. They do not establish that a complete semantic operator existed independently before a learned scaffold.

For training chronology, the [Pythia developmental assay](../../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-scaffold-dynamic-circuits/development/FINDINGS.md) shows a final-selected K/V carrier becoming more effective alongside native competence. It does not measure scaffold onset independently. The [PAN summary](../../../../grok-mechanism-full-proof/RESULTS_SUMMARY.md) reports weak scaffold no later than competent execution in 16 qualified runs, including a synchronous case; strict precedence and scaffold sufficiency are not established. The newer [native role-learning timeline](../../scaffolds-and-dynamic-circuits/figures/native-learning-timeline-FINDINGS.md) also warns that first nonzero effects can be simultaneous by the chain rule and do not date original pretraining acquisition.

## What changes do the circuits make?

Measure a causal chain: source → address/read → writer → carried state → native consumer → later state. Changes can concern the selected address, the value delivered, the residual update, the resulting K/V or recurrent state, and its later consumption. These are distinct observables, even when one intervention moves several together.

The [held-value coding experiment](../../../../saturn/experiments/2026-09-07-qwen-held-value-role-composition-rosetta/FINDINGS.md) transported prompt-derived values across operation boundaries: operation identity remained in the native recipient while value identity traveled through a retained V-row interface. The [apple conversation pilot](../../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-02-apple-scaffold-state-diff/FINDINGS.md) similarly changed a later doubled answer by replacing generated answer-state V/KV, with visible answer IDs fixed. Old prefix rows stayed byte-identical. These observations support content/state transport and changed consumption, rather than parameter updates during inference.

## What can we count?

Declare the unit before counting. Candidate sites, active route supports, and operational causal bundles answer different questions. A bundle has a parent cut, row/address, depth or time support, field, writer/consumer role, and prediction horizon. Test repeatable sufficiency, complementary lesions, repair, wrong source/address/time, coalition effects, and leave-one-out support. Separate shared, substitutable, jointly required, and distributed support.

Three operation names do not imply three circuits. Neither heads, clusters, tensor occupancy, numerical rank, nor a smallest successful transplant supplies a unique endogenous circuit count. The current work can report finite response profiles and a shared interface; it cannot yet report how many independent arithmetic circuits the model contains.
