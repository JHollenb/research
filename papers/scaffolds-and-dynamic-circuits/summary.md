# Scaffolds and Dynamic Circuits Explained

The [architectural paper](architectural-paper.md) develops the idea that models may reuse stable functional roles while different inputs, questions, and carried states determine the semantic computation performed through them. It calls the class **scaffolded dynamic circuits**. The inherited foundation is ordered execution that forms state with later causal authority. The new directed two-update path is a tested recurrent member of that class, while a complete shared graph and autonomous recurrence remain additional hypotheses.

The later [native Mamba operation/clock investigation](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-02-mamba-operation-clock-debugger/FINDINGS.md) now tests two operations on exactly the same country-history state. A frozen 27-coordinate profile beats three fixed competitors on all eight competent held capital rows and four competent delayed identity rows. Recurrent-state-only delivery changes the answer in all 12 qualified rows; immediate-output-only and convolution-only controls retain baseline. Four recent identity rows enter a generic phrase and remain in the evidence. Separating clock retention from fresh writing explains opposing effects that a joint step-size intervention partly hides. This adds predictive reuse within native Mamba, while the complete shared three-family graph remains open.

## The headline news

**A stable internal profile can predict where a new relation computation will run, and newly formed state can causally determine a later answer.**

A Qwen profile frozen over 24 microscopic coordinates predicted spatial-relation effects on eight unopened context combinations: two lexical sets × two templates × two orders. It beat an operation-blind profile, a wrong-operation profile, and a coordinate permutation in all 8/8 combinations. The result is specific rather than universal. Color's reader ordering missed in 8/8 combinations, identity produced registered native answers on only 6/16 binding rows, and the registered size format produced none.

A separate feedback experiment followed state across two updates. In development and one competent held city/country context, the model predicted a value, processed that visible value into a new K/V row, and later consumed the row. With the visible token France held fixed, swapping only the newly formed row for Germany state changed the later answer from France to Germany. Zeroing the row produced `is`; exact reinstall restored France. The other two held contexts did not complete the paired semantic path.

These results connect predictive reuse to downstream semantic authority. They show a recurring interface whose detailed causal profile depends on the operation and learned weight correspondence, and a bounded case where recipient-formed state affects a later native output.

The new [connected-path campaign](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/FINDINGS.md) materially strengthens Mamba. A source/writer map frozen on development transfers to two fresh identity/capital contexts: selected recipient-formed state changes monkey to donkey and produces a Vienna continuation. A follow-up independently replaces the first future state on those same contexts. The identity answer follows the installed state exactly, while restoring baseline state returns monkey even with the coherent current output retained. The capital follow-up generates London versus Vienna in an explicitly diagnostic continuation. This is pretrained native Mamba evidence; supplied spectral addresses and supervised Black Mamba organisms remain separate.

The new Qwen compositions also join route, native state formation, and later score effects. Their stronger forecast remains unestablished: the connected account beats the operation-blind baseline in only 1/3 contexts and ties its additive rival exactly. In the material task, the native recipient chooses `spoon` rather than the registered `shoe`; free `steel` is coherent with that actual choice, while forced `leather` is rank 2 and the registered shoe/leather endpoint is unavailable. These limits do not erase the earlier 8/8 relation result. A separate Pythia checkpoint assay has exact source/state closure but weak semantic answers and a stronger matched-depth control, so acquisition of the proposed architecture remains open.

This C6 composition forecast is distinct from the independent C5 route-attribution packet: C5 establishes exact L22→L23 key/access and L23–L27 future closure, with counteracting L23 and positive L24 contributions, while the fixed bridge and greedy answers remain unchanged.

The [connected SDXL lane](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/diffusion/FINDINGS.md) adds a state-conditioned writer → successor → later writer/RGB path in one development row and one fresh held prompt/seed. Restoring the live writer at every step reproduces the native image exactly. At a single cut, even matching the native writer output leaves different future state and writers, because incoming state remains different. Visible object patterns, shape, contents, and background change; prompt mismatch and collateral prevent an isolated semantic-edit claim. The wrong-sign arm also scores slightly above live on one held pixel metric. These are joined native paths across all three families, with different resolutions and incomplete shared-role alignment.

The [post-hoc repaired E10 consumer/timing rerun](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-completed-answer-rerun/FINDINGS.md) collected all 48 original rows for Qwen2.5-0.5B FP32 and Qwen2.5-1.5B BF16 on already opened inputs. Historical 14-token parser flips remain 32/32 and 30/30, while completed native removal endpoints are wrong on 26/32 versus 3/30, correct repairs on 6/32 versus 25/30, and unresolved at the generation budget on 0/32 versus 2/30. Completed counterfactual swaps author the donor answer on 19/32 versus 30/30. All original primary baselines are correct on 32/32 rows in both models; the two historically excluded Qwen1.5 primary rows stay diagnostic-only and received no interventions.

The later multi-step multiplication is completed correctly on 8/8 in both despite leading-number checks of 0/8 and 3/8. Generated early-removal branches produce wrong completed final-consumer endpoints on 3/8 and 6/8 under the first-result handoff readout. Because a branch can stop before the intended product, these are stopping-stage-confounded handoff diagnostics: a numerically correct sum can still occupy the wrong role, so they do not establish pure P2 formation failure or full autonomous successor selection. Native and donor branches emit the proper product and downstream answer on 8/8 in both; early swaps author counterfactual answers on 8/8, and exact remount reproduces native outputs.

## What the paper argues

A scaffold is the proposed recurring organization of roles: source, selector or live operation, read, writer, carried state, and consumer. A dynamic circuit is the input-specific support and payload used on one execution. The scaffold could stay functionally similar while color, identity, geometry, or relation information changes.

The strongest observed result is state-dependent causal computation in Qwen. Diffusion and Mamba also align prospectively on a smaller common object: family-native formed carried state consumed by an unchanged family-native consumer. That comparison has two FLUX rows and two Mamba rows; a later prospective Qwen appendix contributes one competent row out of three. Its all-row diagnostic reverses the competent-row comparison, so the evidence supports a partial core rather than a complete common graph. Pythia separately shows that task-relevant causal organization changes across training checkpoints.

The new feedback packet goes beyond recording an appended cache row: it independently replaces, zeros, and reinstalls the second formed row and measures the later answer. Its scope is controlled and host-orchestrated. Queries, donors, and branch selection are supplied, the intervention spans all 28 layers, and the experiment neither localizes a minimal writer nor establishes a donor-free autonomous loop.

## Why it matters

The scientific opportunity is to move from a catalog of components associated with behaviors toward a causal account of **how stored state and the current operation organize computation**. That could make circuit discovery more predictive: a role map would tell us what a new context should read, where it should write, and what an intervention should change.

For the scene generator, the diffusion findings explain why an edit may need contextual state rather than the changed word alone, and why current conditioning and accumulated scene state must be controlled separately. A validated scaffold could support more reusable semantic controls. This campaign does not yet supply a learned general compiler or prove cross-family portability.

The paper's present contribution is a bounded architectural result: a prior-fixed relation profile predicts new contexts, recipient-formed state can control a later semantic answer, and native read/write/state paths are now joined within Qwen, Mamba, and SDXL at different resolutions. Three substrates share formation followed by future consumption; the full common role graph remains incomplete. Strong final-weight disorder shows dependence on current Qwen value-projection correspondence, while chronology remains open. A fuller scaffold account needs a discriminating joint prediction and prospective alignment of the still-unmatched source/operation/read/write roles. Learning origin and automatic addressing require their own evidence if claimed. Unique minimal writers and autonomous control are additional properties; they are not prerequisites for the inherited time-formed foundation or the bounded result.

Raw terminal statuses remain unassessed. The paper reports these measurements as bounded observations and trends rather than treating the mechanics verifier as a scientific terminal bundle.
