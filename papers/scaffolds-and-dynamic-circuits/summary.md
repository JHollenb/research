# Scaffolds and Dynamic Circuits Explained

The [architectural paper](architectural-paper.md) develops the idea that models may reuse stable functional roles while different inputs, questions, and carried states determine the semantic computation performed through them. It calls this bounded architecture **scaffolded recurrent semantic circuits**. The evidence now supports several connected roles and one directed two-update path, while a complete shared graph and autonomous recurrence remain hypotheses.

## The headline news

**A stable internal profile can predict where a new relation computation will run, and newly formed state can causally determine a later answer.**

A Qwen profile frozen over 24 microscopic coordinates predicted spatial-relation effects on eight unopened context combinations: two lexical sets × two templates × two orders. It beat an operation-blind profile, a wrong-operation profile, and a coordinate permutation in all 8/8 combinations. The result is specific rather than universal. Color's reader ordering missed in 8/8 combinations, identity produced registered native answers on only 6/16 binding rows, and the registered size format produced none.

A separate feedback experiment followed state across two updates. In development and one competent held city/country context, the model predicted a value, processed that visible value into a new K/V row, and later consumed the row. With the visible token France held fixed, swapping only the newly formed row for Germany state changed the later answer from France to Germany. Zeroing the row produced `is`; exact reinstall restored France. The other two held contexts did not complete the paired semantic path.

These results connect predictive reuse to downstream semantic authority. They show a recurring interface whose detailed causal profile depends on the operation and learned weight correspondence, and a bounded case where recipient-formed state affects a later native output.

## What the paper argues

A scaffold is the proposed recurring organization of roles: source, selector or live operation, read, writer, carried state, and consumer. A dynamic circuit is the input-specific support and payload used on one execution. The scaffold could stay functionally similar while color, identity, geometry, or relation information changes.

The strongest observed result is state-dependent causal computation in Qwen. Diffusion and Mamba also align prospectively on a smaller common object: family-native formed carried state consumed by an unchanged family-native consumer. That comparison has two FLUX rows and two Mamba rows; a later prospective Qwen appendix contributes one competent row out of three. Its all-row diagnostic reverses the competent-row comparison, so the evidence supports a partial core rather than a complete common graph. Pythia separately shows that task-relevant causal organization changes across training checkpoints.

The new feedback packet goes beyond recording an appended cache row: it independently replaces, zeros, and reinstalls the second formed row and measures the later answer. Its scope is controlled and host-orchestrated. Queries, donors, and branch selection are supplied, the intervention spans all 28 layers, and the experiment neither localizes a minimal writer nor establishes a donor-free autonomous loop.

The mrun campaign contains **thirteen successful evidence packages**, including one mechanics reproduction that adds no fresh scientific examples. It used resident models, immutable parent reuse, qualified batching, and sequential GPU panels. Successful canonical job times were 11.39–135.04 seconds, summing to 462.01 seconds; failed and earlier qualification history is excluded. The separately completed Qwen context study has its own jobs and is outside that count.

A campaign-level check verified all thirteen historical packages with zero mechanics or custody errors. The later confirmation campaign has a separate 15-run index, including two retained failed attempts; its verifier checked 115 indexed references with zero errors and reports valid mechanics. Verification protects the reliability of the record. It does not certify the architectural hypothesis, semantic universality, or publication readiness.

An older Qwen feedback packet used a defective custom causal-mask path, so its multi-token native-feedback interpretation remains historically qualified. The new result comes from a separately sealed fused TokenMachine packet that derives and passes native causal masks and verifies same-parent observer parity. It does not retroactively repair the old receipt.

## Why it matters

The scientific opportunity is to move from a catalog of components associated with behaviors toward a causal account of **how stored state and the current operation organize computation**. That could make circuit discovery more predictive: a role map would tell us what a new context should read, where it should write, and what an intervention should change.

For the scene generator, the diffusion findings explain why an edit may need contextual state rather than the changed word alone, and why current conditioning and accumulated scene state must be controlled separately. A validated scaffold could support more reusable semantic controls. This campaign does not yet supply a learned general compiler or prove cross-family portability.

The paper's present contribution is a bounded architectural result: a prior-fixed relation profile predicts new contexts, recipient-formed state can control a later semantic answer, and three substrates share a partial formed-state-to-consumer pattern. Strong final-weight disorder shows that the measured Qwen profile depends on current L22/L23 value-projection correspondence, although the simultaneous competence loss and post-held design prevent a learning-history claim. A reusable full graph with minimal writers, broad feedback replication, learning-time acquisition, automatic addressing, and repeated autonomous closure remains the larger result.

Raw terminal statuses remain unassessed. The paper reports these measurements as bounded observations and trends rather than treating the mechanics verifier as a scientific terminal bundle.
