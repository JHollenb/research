# Architecture experiment instrument audit — October 1, 2026

The reviewed evidence supports real, bounded effects of source state, formed carriers, routes, and native consumers. It does not justify treating every reported success as a completed semantic answer, every failed diagnostic as a mechanism failure, or every later experiment as a repair of the same earlier instrument. The most consequential newly identified readout issue is in E10: a finite rollout's arithmetic operand can be reported as its final answer. The review also identified latent delivery/status/custody defects and qualifications to cross-family comparison; collected positive mechanics and family-local effects survive within the scopes below.

This audit reads source, raw reports, intervention definitions, manifests, custody, verification, and later follow-ups. It launches no model runs. The [48-hour coverage audit](experiment-coverage-audit-2026-10-01.md) remains the broader inventory; coverage is not a claim that every adjacent package received the same source-level review. The architecture core, connected paths, first-wave comparisons, and principal formation/read/CoT predecessors receive the detailed review here. Historical raw results, frozen workers, rejected futures, and earlier verifier outcomes remain unchanged.

**Subsequent E10 model reproduction:** after this source audit, the [mathematical diagnosis and completed-answer rerun](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-completed-answer-rerun/FINDINGS.md) collected both original 48-row panels through mrun. Primary removal ends wrong on 26/32 and 3/30 historically admitted rows, repairs on 6/32 and 25/30, and leaves two Qwen1.5 endpoints unresolved. The saved multiplication checks reproduce the leading-number flag pattern but complete correctly on 8/8 rows per model, answering the specific 5/8 concern below. All main primary removal/swap short strings match the historical strings; the complete historical-string comparison is 320/320 for Qwen0.5 and 304/310 for Qwen1.5, with six optional truncation differences. The original unresolved-search statements below describe evidence available before this new reproduction; generated-chain stopping-stage and probability-conditioning limits remain explicit in the new findings.

## Findings and surviving claims

### 1. E10's parser overstates completed-answer failure

**Severity: high for the final-answer claim; the intervention evidence remains useful.** The executed worker hash matches the declared source hashes in both full E10 receipts. Its [14-token rollout and parser](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-cot-intermediate-steps/worker_e10.py) select the first number after the last `=`. In **8/32** Qwen0.5 and **24/30** Qwen1.5 primary removal rows, the saved text ends with a compound RHS such as `6 times 7 - 5 = 42 - 5`. The reported `42` is an exposed operand, not a completed final result.

The historical 32/32 and 30/30 rates therefore measure bounded parser-readout changes, including incomplete expressions. The continuous metric scores the whole baseline continuation prefix through its selected number, including any worked arithmetic. It is not an isolated final-answer logprob.

**What survives:** directed swaps author the correct donor arithmetic answer on **15/32** and **30/30** primary rows; all scored native donors match their arithmetic targets. Native distributions change under formed-state intervention with visible scaffold text fixed. All 440 saved step-arm readouts reproduce the legacy parser and flags; consistency is not semantic validity. The separate scope audit checks **628** base, step-arm, and whole-path strings.

**Correction:** [readout qualification and lineage](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-cot-intermediate-steps/READOUT-QUALIFICATION-2026-10-01.md), [recomputable string audit](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-cot-intermediate-steps/audit_readout.py), and [immutable additive observations](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-cot-intermediate-steps/results/corrections/2026-10-01-architecture-audit/readout-scope.json). Current paper, summary, architecture draft, and outline disclose the bounded endpoint and longer reproduction. Historical findings carry a qualification backlink. Completed-answer rates cannot be inferred from these original finite strings; the subsequent native consumer assay now measures them separately, as documented in the update above.

### 2. E10's supplied successor is not regenerated after early removal

**Severity: medium for the claimed temporal mechanism; already recognized in the earlier verification.** The entire scratchpad is prefilled before intervention. Early removal changes 0/8 readouts, while later-step and whole-path removal change 8/8. The later state was already cached. This supports downstream use of that state, without establishing its regeneration after removal. A decisive later singleton also prevents the strict path-distributed step-grain certificate. Remount records sequence-logprob equality; it does not retain bytewise state equality or a separate remount rollout. These qualifications were already in the revised space/time manuscript; stale architecture prose has now been reconciled with them.

### 3. Acquisition alignment status and held-freeze binding repaired

**Severity: medium, prospective integrity defect.** The [acquisition worker](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/acquisition/worker.py) could emit `native-aligned` independently of its recorded parity and state checks. The verifier printed failures without making its exit or row status reflect them, and the analyzer trusted the label. Held submission also checked the manifest digest without binding the complete development freeze, predictions, row IDs, and layer policy into executed custody.

All currently collected mechanics checks are true and the collected freeze is consistent. These defects therefore do not invalidate the existing signed checkpoint trends. The current [model-free contract](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/acquisition/contract.py), worker, launcher, verifier, and analyzer now derive status from named integrity checks; bind freeze bytes, development report bytes/seal, IDs, prediction, tokenizer, and layer policy; and retain invalid rows while separating attribution eligibility. New executions require complete binding. Historical held custody is explicitly `legacy-compatible`, rather than represented as having the new request fields. Additive [development](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/acquisition/results/corrections/2026-10-01-instrument-audit/development-verification.json) and [held](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/acquisition/results/corrections/2026-10-01-instrument-audit/held-verification.json) checks retain 4/4 and 16/16 mechanically valid rows. The new [analysis](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/acquisition/results/corrections/2026-10-01-instrument-audit/analysis.json) retains all 20 rows and signed effects. Mixed validity remains visible in the trend status. Eight adversarial/integration tests pass, including repeated checkpoint IDs.

Only six native/primary arms determine attribution applicability. Optional wrong-sign, matched-depth, K/V-only and successor diagnostics retain per-arm completeness and failure reasons; a diagnostic failure cannot erase valid primary comparisons. This is an instrument correction, not a scientific effect gate.

### 4. Historical custom-mask defect has a specific corrective lineage

The later [C5 route/write/future attribution](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-qwen-route-write-future-attribution/FINDINGS.md) supplies a distinct frozen development/held experiment. It has native parity, reciprocal state closure, staged K/V delivery, and independently reconstructed raw mediator tensors. It closes a bounded route-produced future-state edge that the older proximal K/V projection alone did not establish. Keep old mask-confounded numbers out of numerical comparisons with the corrected instrument.

### 7. E2 and C4 normalized summaries need their raw denominators and physical meaning

E2's continuation ratios are signed paired-effect ratios, not bounded mediation percentages. The corrected eight-row packet has positive seed gains, including a color denominator of .1505 nats; rescue ratios include negative effects and values above one. Those are observations to retain. Eight-token match scores are quantized: one color row has filler match .875 and observed match .375, producing normalized effect **−4.0**. Perfect filler match would make normalization undefined; all eight corrected rows have finite denominators. A median of zero does not erase the negative counter-trend. Report numerator, denominator, raw continuation, and finite/undefined counts together; no arbitrary effect floor is added as a discovery gate.

C4 compares family-local interventions with different recipient baselines and endpoints. FLUX rescues target latent under base conditioning and measures RGB distance; Mamba rescues target state in a generic parent and measures answer log-probability margins. Their four-component RMSE is a descriptive normalized-profile comparison, not calibrated common causal strength or a matched recipient factorial. The joint-parent coordinate is fixed at one. The frozen permutation also exchanges that constant with the measured wrong-coordinate effect, so its poor score includes the scale-reference construction. Four components are not four independent replications: each primary family has two held rows. The Qwen appendix already separates its one competent row from the all-three-row diagnostic, whose tool/material wrong-depth ratio reaches 27.231; both views remain useful within those labels.

The later E1b relation diagnostic is a real correction in address **scope**: the frozen lexical-row policy misses (−.045 RGB progress), while whole active text reaches +.882 and row permutation +.054. The complement reaches +.725 with collateral. It does not turn the original held lexical-row miss into a pass.

The additive [comparative qualification](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-scaffold-confirmation/QUALIFICATION-2026-10-01.md) and [per-row derivation](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-scaffold-confirmation/QUALIFICATION-2026-10-01.json) retain signed numerators/denominators, finite and undefined counts, recipient/endpoints, component-versus-row counts, and source byte hashes. They change interpretation without filtering scientific counter-trends or rewriting the executed comparison.

### 8. Connected freeze lineage now binds completed development evidence

**Severity: medium, prospective custody defect.** The existing Qwen, Mamba, and SDXL freezes bind protocol/model/input identities under their original checks, but do not establish the stronger contract of retaining and binding the completed development report's exact bytes. A canonical report seal and a raw-file SHA are different identities; legacy fields must not silently satisfy both obligations.

The current lane-specific [Qwen](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/qwen/freeze_lineage.py), [Mamba](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/mamba/freeze_lineage.py), and [SDXL](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/diffusion/freeze_lineage.py) helpers require explicit `development_report_file_sha256` and `development_report_seal_sha256` when applicable, exact freeze bytes, completed development schema/phase, model identity, row IDs, and prediction source. Launchers stage the retained evidence; workers and independent verifiers check it. A prospective marker with a deleted required digest fails instead of falling back to historical compatibility. The SDXL helper itself is now included in source custody. Helpers load under distinct path-based module names, preventing one lane from accidentally consuming another lane's cached helper.

Additive held verification preserves valid recorded mechanics for [Qwen](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/qwen/results/corrections/2026-10-01-instrument-audit/freeze-held-verification.json), [Mamba](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/mamba/results/corrections/2026-10-01-instrument-audit/held-verification.json), and [SDXL](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/diffusion/results/corrections/2026-10-01-instrument-audit/held-verification-v2.json), with explicit **`historical-unbound`** development-report lineage. SDXL's [development check](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-connected-architecture/diffusion/results/corrections/2026-10-01-instrument-audit/development-verification-v2.json) labels the held-only lineage obligation `not-applicable`. Thirteen lineage regressions pass, including mutated/missing/ambiguous identities, schema corruption, legacy seal disagreement, and downgrade attempts. A single-process import check resolves each lane's own helper. An artifact-only SDXL conversion validates the new byte binding, but retains historical development source hashes. That converted freeze cannot be admitted under the changed worker source. The launcher now rejects that mismatch before submission or dry-run completion, matching the worker check; it does not relabel historical source bytes as current.

Mamba's requirement for vocabulary-wide native correctness across every operation family is also demoted to a semantic applicability diagnostic. Per-operation competence and raw qualification flags remain visible. An unavailable semantic readout does not block mechanically valid state evidence or lineage collection; claims about correct semantic behavior still require competent native comparisons.

These repairs qualify future collection. Adding a seal after collection cannot establish earlier freeze chronology, make opened held inputs fresh, or retroactively measure missing tensors. A new prospective claim must use the appropriate development source/protocol identity and unopened held inputs. Existing reports, freezes, executed source snapshots, and historical verifier outcomes remain unchanged.

## Offline verification and limits

The [historical check receipt](experiment-instrument-checks-2026-10-01.json) records E20's 6/6 artifact checks and four independent corruption fixtures: missing row, corrupted effect ratio, nonfinite observation, and missing log marker. Each fixture was rejected using a temporary copy; the historical report hash stayed unchanged. Six readout examples distinguish completed numeric candidates from compound RHSs, missing numbers, and unparsed text. That receipt's E10 block covers only the original reports and their 224/216 step-arm readouts, not the new reproduction. It remains unchanged. The new [Qwen0.5 verification](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-completed-answer-rerun/results/qwen05-full/run-527c598e9bc0/verification.json) and [Qwen1.5 verification](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-01-e10-completed-answer-rerun/results/qwen15-full/run-d637c4110a59/verification.json) separately check the rerun's custody, required cache mechanics, and 808/786 completed readouts.

The newer SDXL development and held artifacts independently pass all ten declared checks, including 18 image records and 60 snapshot-file hashes across the two runs. The retained source/request hashes match their executed snapshots. These later results replace the initially inspected calibration-only status. Image checks certify actual PNG/RGB artifacts and replay, not isolated semantic correctness. The held global-cosine wrong-sign score (.8293) exceeds matched (.8274), whereas its ROI distance is worse (39.85 versus 36.45); that disagreement is retained.

The corrected instruments pass **40 regression cases**: six E10 readout, eight Qwen delivery/integrity, thirteen shared-lane lineage, eight acquisition contract, and five comparative qualification cases. Tiny delivery tests use CPU tensors without constructing a model. C1's additive logical-lane audit, the cross-lane import check, and historical artifact verification are separate checks. No model job or training was launched by this audit.

Artifact verifiers establish execution identity, delivery, recorded arithmetic, and declared mechanics within their scope. They cannot recover tensors or checking rollouts that were not retained, infer eventual answers from truncated text, turn a supplied donor into an autonomous resolver, or prove a physical relativity law. No single status or aggregate score should be used as authority for all those claims.
