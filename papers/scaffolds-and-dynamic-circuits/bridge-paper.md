# Scaffolds and Dynamic Circuits: Functional Role Organization Across Formed State

Jacob Hollenbeck

*Draft 1, 2026-10-01; companion to [Time-Formed Circuits in Transformers](../space-and-time-circuits/time-emphasis-paper.md).*

## Abstract

The companion paper identifies a circuit class whose controlling state is formed through ordered execution. This paper asks what reusable organization makes those circuits possible across changing contents and operations. We propose a functional scaffold: recurring source, address/read, writer, carried-state, and consumer responsibilities whose semantic support is instantiated by the current context and history. In Qwen2.5-1.5B, changing only the native question changes the causal authority of a byte-identical intervention in the same parent K/V store. Query-selective harm is positive in 16/16 color and 16/16 position contrasts, with medians 3.130 and 0.583 nats, and a recipient's unchanged suffix can turn a supplied L12 identity residual into a late record that independently rescues identity in 16/16 generated reads.

A discovery-frozen 24-coordinate reader profile then predicts unopened spatial-relation effects across all eight combinations of two lexical sets, two templates, and two orders, beating three frozen competitors in every combination. Color retains partial profile similarity but misses its coarse reader prediction in 8/8 combinations; identity is sparsely readable, and size is unavailable under the registered held format. Mild 5% and 20% row permutations in L22/L23 `v_proj` largely preserve the profiles, whereas permuting all 256 rows and matched biases changes mapped cosine from .931 to .569 for identity, .838 to .110 for color, and .955 to .049 for relation while native competence also falls. This establishes dependence of the measured profile on final-checkpoint value-projection correspondence, not when that organization was acquired.

Newly formed state also has later semantic authority. In development and one competent held city/country context of three, native predictions are processed into successive all-layer K/V rows; with the first state, visible intermediate token, prefix, later query, and recipient final hidden state fixed, replacing only the second formed row changes the later semantic answer, zeroing it breaks the endpoint, and exact reinstall restores it. The connected follow-up joins a native Qwen route to a recipient-formed successor and a later score on three fresh contexts, while a separate route-attribution packet identifies L23 key-mediated access, a counteracting L23 answer contribution, positive L24 contribution, and exact L23–L27 closure with unchanged greedy answers. The connected forecast is modest (MAE .015099 versus .014593 operation-blind, one of three wins, and an exact additive tie), so it limits a stronger graph-level prediction claim without removing the causal path. The execution is host-orchestrated and target-assisted, not autonomous. The completed connected SDXL lane adds a bounded state-conditioned source/read → cut-4 writer → scheduler successor → later-writer → RGB path on one development row and one fresh held prompt/seed: full-live reinstall is exact at PNG/RGB across all 28 steps, while stale W4 can match the immediate native writer and later rows diverge. Recognizable clock/bowl content and RGB factors retain count, material, contents, and background collateral, so this is not isolated semantic evidence. Diffusion and Mamba provide complementary source, binding, and carried-state evidence; a new Mamba-130M lane joins L0 source, L18–20 S1, native S2 formation, and later continuation on two fresh held contexts, with a `n_new=0` companion closing full-state S2 mediation. A four-date × four-checkpoint Pythia lane closes source-write and successor-cache mechanics but has weak semantic endpoints and a stronger matched-depth L2 control. The prospectively frozen FLUX/Mamba profile and a later one-row competent Qwen appendix support the narrower common edge `family-native formed state -> unchanged family-native consumer`. The results support recurring interfaces with context-specific causal support. A complete microscopic cross-family role graph, unique writers, autonomous addressing, original Qwen acquisition chronology and learning-time universalization remain open. C7/C8 below add coarse connected alignment and controlled continuation.

The [native Mamba operation/clock follow-up](../../../saturn/experiments/2026-10-02-mamba-operation-clock-debugger/FINDINGS.md) adds a same-history operation crossing and prospective microscopic recurrence: a frozen 27-coordinate profile predicts two new country pairs across two delays, beating three competitors in eight capital and four exact-qualified delayed identity rows. Recurrent-only successor delivery changes the answer in all 12 qualified rows. Four recent identity rows enter a generic phrase and remain in the report. Retention-only and write-only clock doses can oppose one another, while reader effects change with operation and token boundary. This supplies a more specific native Mamba bridge from carried history to selective computation; C7 now extends the coarse common graph, and C8 adds a native continuation timeline; complete microscopic alignment and original learning chronology remain separate.

C7 and C8 now add connected coarse graph and controlled native learning evidence. C7 prospectively exercises conditional native computation → writer → formed state → future in Qwen, Mamba and SDXL, retaining old-state paths and a composite diffusion reader. ## 1. From time-formed state to a functional scaffold

The decisive example is deliberately small. In the Qwen context experiment, two branches share the same parent StateCut and the same donor K/V patch. One branch asks for the color of the patched animal; the other asks for the color of the other animal. The patch bytes, layer band, token positions, and tensor digests are identical. Only the native query changes. The patch changes the answer much more when its fact is queried. Holding the stored intervention fixed separates the authority of the formed record from the operation that consumes it. A conventional query-dependent lookup is a possible implementation; the result identifies a recurring causal interface and selective consumption without yet mapping its internal algorithm.

The [first paper](../space-and-time-circuits/time-emphasis-paper.md) follows the construction of the record: source → native formation → committed state → consumer. This paper follows its use across operations. The same formed store can host different facts, and a changed question can make different support causally relevant. The response-specific circuit is the active read, payload, and downstream operation within that execution, rather than a permanent semantic module assigned to one word.

The scaffold question is architectural: which responsibilities recur when those dynamic computations change? A source supplies contextual operands, a live read or transition selects and transforms them, a writer commits state, and an unchanged native consumer produces the result. Recurrence of those causal roles on unopened contexts can support a functional organization, provided the role assignments are measured and frozen before evaluation. In the hypothesis, the reusable organization is learned across training; its contextual state and active support are instantiated during inference. Those two clocks must remain separate.

The proposed bridge from the two papers is therefore:

```text
time-formed causal state  ->  recurring functional roles  ->  context-specific dynamic circuit
source and trajectory         interface and recurrence       payload, address, dose, timing, support
```

Source-to-store and trajectory interventions support the first arrow. The second requires recurrence across contexts or operations with frozen selection; the third requires same-parent native-consumer effects because changing activations alone is generic. These remain separate evidence levels.

The claim ladder is explicit: **measured**, a reused late interface supports several contents and operations with query-conditioned native effects, its frozen relation profile predicts unopened contexts, and one bounded two-update path gives newly formed state later semantic authority; **convergent trend**, formed state reaches unchanged native consumers across Qwen, FLUX, and Mamba in family-local forms; **working inference**, these recurring responsibilities are parts of a learned reusable scaffold with broader carried-state feedback; **measured additions**, a coarse C7 connected graph and C8 native continuation under original consumers; **open**, original pretraining acquisition, automatic addressing, a complete microscopic cross-family graph and an exhaustive spatial/temporal taxonomy. The prompt-built object is the contextual state and the circuit active on it; the reusable scaffold is the organization hypothesized to make those instances possible.

## 2. What the scaffold is

Let a family-local execution expose candidate sites `V_m` and causal relations `E_m`. A scaffold hypothesis is a role graph

\[
G^*=(R,E^*), \qquad R=\{\text{source},\text{operation/address},\text{carrier/transition},\text{writer},\text{carried state},\text{consumer}\},
\]

with a family-local lowering `L_m:R→V_m`. The role names acquire scientific content from signed native-consumer interventions. A source or store lesion plus a downstream rescue can establish a mediator at the tested cut. A replacement or write assay can establish only the causal role it actually measures. Shape, layer order, an attention label, or a convenient row name is a hypothesis about a role, not evidence that the role owns a semantic operation.

A reusable causal interface earns a predictive functional account when a frozen role/site/edge profile predicts new causal effects beyond declared generic alternatives. The new relation result does so at its tested resolution; color and identity are heterogeneous. Final-weight disorder separately demonstrates dependence on the correspondence of two selected value projections. Attributing this organization to a particular learning process or checkpoint stage needs an acquisition comparison. These are distinct claims, rather than a retrospective gate on interface reuse.

Responsibilities recurring across executions and carried-state feedback are separate claims. The first is supported at several bounded interfaces; selected read/write assays support parts of the second. Here `t` denotes a declared family-local update axis: an autoregressive query/write event, a diffusion denoising call, or an SSM recurrent transition. It does not denote a shared clock or tensor type. The proposed organization has the compact transition

```text
context and current operation
              |
              v
carried state_(t-1) -> live address or transition read -> semantic writer
                                                            |
                                                            v
                 unchanged native consumer <- carried state_t
                                                            |
                                                            - -> next read or transition
                                                                 (bounded assays; open in general)
```

The **static role scaffold** is the recurring organization of those possible roles and edges. It is not a constant tensor and it is not simply the engineered module graph. The **dynamic semantic subcircuit** is the context- and history-dependent support instantiated on one execution. A useful record is `D_{m,c,o,t}=(A,P,alpha,tau,M)`, where `A` is source or address selection, `P` is the payload, `alpha` is dose or gain, `tau` is timing and order, and `M⊆V_m` is the family-local support that the native consumer responds to. Semantic interpretation rests on directed native-consumer effects evaluated alongside the parentage, donor, competence, and counterfactual controls appropriate to that assay. Same-parent contrasts are preferred when the question permits them because they isolate the changed operand most sharply.

This vocabulary separates four easily conflated objects.

| Object | What remains stable | What can vary during an execution | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Static weights** | learned parameters and numerical runtime | activations, state, and outputs | weight custody; this alone says nothing about a scaffold |
| **Contextual K/V or source products** | a prompt-conditioned source at a declared boundary, sometimes fixed for one run | the live query, residual, address, and downstream state | same-parent K/V/Q or source/write factorial through the native consumer |
| **Dynamic graph or subcircuit** | recurring functional roles and perhaps a route family | active sites, payloads, signs, doses, timing, and recipients | frozen discovery, held contexts or operations, wrong-source and timing controls |
| **Persistent carried register** | a state interface that survives one update to the next | the state contents and how later operations read it | state-only, program-only, and joint interventions with exact replay |

The word **hardening** refers to acquisition of persistent causal authority in the carried state. A transient source can influence later consumers because a suffix forms a record, latent, cache, or recurrent state with independently testable authority. Cache persistence alone does not establish semantic commitment. Hardening can be partial, overwritten, redundant, or repaired. It is not online weight modification or absolute irreversibility: later sources can rewrite the state, alternate routes can preserve a factor, and queries can render a store irrelevant.

This also explains why the observer's symbol table is not a native symbol table. A row number, token span, head list, or source address is an instrument coordinate supplied by a particular assay. In the scene-editing experiment, prompt row numbers aligned exact prompt grammars but were explicitly not permanent semantic addresses, and the complete contextual text stream was transplanted. The `ExecutionSkeleton` in the predecessor paper is tensor-free and non-authoritative; its `DynamicCircuitRecord` is evidence pinned to that skeleton. A native symbol table would require a model-internal, portable mapping from semantic names to operands and consumers. The current evidence does not provide one.

## 3. Qwen: interface reuse, predictive recurrence, and formed-state feedback

### 3.1 One interface, different contents and questions

The latest Qwen scaffold-context panel supplies the strongest same-parent test in this paper. It uses frozen Qwen2.5-1.5B, four lexical scenes, 64 correlated query rows, 1,024 private-cache branches, and 4,048 physical forwards. The L22–27 interface was inherited from E20 and fixed before this campaign. Fox/cat served as the mechanics/discovery pilot; wolf/bear, horse/dog, and lion/tiger then used the same frozen worker, layer bands, operators, and readouts. These are correlated panel replications, not four independent held-out tests. The native consumer generated its own continuations rather than being teacher-forced. The raw findings and all row observations are retained in [QWEN-SCF].

Replacing the queried late K/V fact span with a coherent donor state redirected the native answer across multiple operations. Color transferred to the donor full-vocabulary top choice in 16/16 rows. Position transferred in 12/16. Identity produced the donor animal in all 11 available patched reads; five of the 16 eligible continuations did not emit a registered animal within four tokens and remain unavailable rather than being counted as semantic wins or failures. The position-to-color row is a useful diagnostic, with donor color outranking the target in 16/16 but becoming top choice in only 7/16. The same late interface therefore supports several operations without establishing an identical microscopic circuit for each one.

The decisive contrast fixes the stored intervention. For each scene and query pair, the two forks share the exact parent StateCut, donor input, layer band, positions, K/V values, and tensor digests. The native question changes. Query-selective harm is positive in all 16/16 color scene/order/binding contrasts, with median 3.130 nats, and all 16/16 position contrasts, with median 0.583 nats. The position-to-color diagnostic is also positive in 16/16, with median 3.021 nats. There are 128 exact directional patch-matching checks, but only 32 unique same-parent query pairs; the larger number certifies payload matching rather than independent replication. This is query-conditioned causal consumption, not independent mediation: the same parent state and intervention bytes have different behavioral consequences because the current query changes their use.

Identity adds a formation path. A clean animal-anchor residual is seeded at layer 12 into a donor recipient, which then runs its own remaining layers. The recipient forms its own late layer-22–27 record. Installing that recipient-formed anchor into an unseeded recipient rescues target identity in 16/16 generated reads; replacing it with unseeded state restores the donor identity in 14/14 available reads. The bounded chain is therefore

```text
supplied clean residual at L12 -> recipient-native remaining layers
        -> recipient-formed L22–27 K/V
        -> independent transplant into an unseeded recipient
        -> query-conditioned native answer
```

The proposed mediator is recipient-formed after a supplied clean source residual, rather than copied from a completed clean donor trace. The experiment is donor-assisted and uses host-supplied source addresses. It does not show that color and position use the same formation path. Position already responds at an earlier band in 9/16 rows. A later microscopic packet maps shared L23/KV0/V and L22/KV1/V influence and GQA-group participation; its frozen relation profile transfers prospectively. Individual necessary heads, complete writer edges, and the query-to-source algorithm remain unresolved. The recurring interface is supported without automatic semantic addressing or unique minimality.

The older E20 mediation panel makes source-to-store quantitative. Across 42 identity rows, blocking the L22–27 band formed by the recipient after the supplied L12 residual removed a median 0.924 of that seed's answer-logprob gain, while transplanting the seeded recipient's formed band into an unseeded recipient recovered 0.871. A wrong identity's band authored the wrong identity on 37/42 rows. This independent block/rescue chain connects source, formed store, and consumer while retaining signed over-removal and over-rescue rows; it does not make the band unique or universal. See [E20].

### 3.2 A frozen reader profile predicts unopened relation contexts

The microscopic discovery packet resolves a 24-coordinate signed profile over L22–27, two K/V heads, and K/V contributions. The confirmation packet froze the operation-specific map together with three competitors—an operation-blind mean, a cyclic wrong-operation profile, and a within-band coordinate permutation—before opening two lexical sets × two templates × two orders. Up to two bindings are averaged within context before scoring. Competence-conditioned summaries retain only applicable bindings and take the median across context-level scores: 15 bindings across eight contexts for color and relation, and six bindings across four contexts for identity. The broadest context denominator is eight correlated combinations rather than the 4,736 receipted intervention lanes.

Spatial relation supplies the clean predictive result. The mapped profile beats all three frozen competitors in raw RMSE in 8/8 context combinations. On the competent context summary its cosine is .954, compared with .566 for the operation-blind vector, and its raw RMSE is .029, compared with .149. Color is a partial transfer: its competent cosine is .839 versus .828 operation-blind, but the blind profile has slightly lower raw error, .109 versus .119, and the predicted Q6–11 reader advantage misses in 8/8 combinations. Identity is favorable only in the all-row view; among the four contexts with any registered identity continuation, the operation-blind comparator is better. Size has 0/16 registered native answers in the frozen held format and is instrument-unavailable. Development query calibration shows that a direct-adjective size format can work, but it does not reinterpret the held rows. The result is prospective recurrence for relation, partial structure for color, and readout-limited evidence for identity and size—not a universal operation map.

The same packet separates current trained organization from acquisition history. Permuting 5% and 20% of the output rows and matched biases in L22/L23 `v_proj` mostly preserves the profiles. A separately sealed post-held arm permutes all 256 rows and biases in those two projections, preserving shapes and norms and restoring the original weights exactly. Across the same four opened contexts, mapped cosine changes from .931/.924/.905/.569 across doses 0/.05/.20/1.0 for identity, .838/.834/.794/.110 for color, and .955/.952/.946/.049 for relation. Native competence also falls at full disruption, and relation still retains a lower median raw error than the blind profile even though its signed shape collapses. The narrow conclusion is dependence of the measured causal profile on final-checkpoint L22/L23 value-projection correspondence. This is not a whole-model shuffle, a fully behavior-matched null, or evidence about acquisition chronology [CONFIRM].

### 3.3 Successive formed rows have later semantic authority

The feedback packet tests whether written semantic state participates in another update. On development, native execution produces `cat -> blue -> blue`. Replacing the complete newly formed first K/V row with coherent fox state changes the next prediction and final answer to `red -> red`. After native processing of that visible `red` token forms a second row, replacing only that second row with clean blue state changes the later answer to `blue`; zeroing it produces `is`, and exact reinstall restores `red`. The intervention spans the newly staged row across all 28 layers. L27 names the resumable final-layer cut, not a sole writer.

The frozen city/country context repeats the complete path. Native Berlin state yields `Germany -> Germany`, and Paris state yields `France -> France`. A coherent first-row swap makes the next native prediction France. With that first state, the visible France token, prefix, final hidden state, and later query fixed, replacing only the newly formed France row with Germany state changes the later answer from France to Germany; reinstall restores France. Fruit/shape has large causal state effects but an incompetent base chain and serial final, while tool/material never forms its declared target. The complete held semantic result is therefore 1/3 contexts.

Semantic authority comes from fused `TokenMachine` native-layer execution through unchanged remaining blocks and LM head. The same-parent decomposed L22 observer has exact logits, K/V, and token parity at its capture; the packet does not claim parity of every cycle with one monolithic `model.forward` call. Prompts, known query suffixes, donors, branch choices, and interventions are host supplied. The result closes a bounded `S1 -> native S2 prediction -> formed S2 state -> later answer` path while allowing parallel routes; it does not establish autonomy, automatic addressing, or a unique L4/L22/L23 writer-reader chain. Its raw terminal status remains unassessed [CONFIRM].

### 3.4 Comparative transformer results and instrument qualifications

The prospective E2 Qwen2.5-0.5B role map supplies a useful countertrend. A residual writer at layer 2 and a K/V writer at layer 20 transferred from identity discovery to disjoint identity and color contexts. In the recipient-formed-store extension after the supplied L2 seed, the late block reduced seeded answer evidence on all eight rows, and recipient-formed-store rescue increased identity answer evidence on 4/4 rows with median recovery 0.793 of the seeded answer gain. It improved the clean eight-token continuation on only 2/4 rows. Color rescue improved answer evidence on only 2/4 and produced no continuation-match gain. The K/V-cut arm was non-specific: a filler construction made row shuffles identical on 44/44 examples, and a wrong source layer was stronger than the selected source on most rows. The result preserves a causal carrier trend while the instrument does not establish exclusive mediation.

The selector campaign asks whether carried state changes a later read and write. A subsequent audit found that the historical 0.5B and 1.5B attention wrappers lacked matching causal-mask registration; their multi-token routing, appended-K/V, competence, and continuation measurements are not qualified native causal evidence. Historical effects remain recorded under that instrument. A corrected 1.5B follow-up retains the L4 selector, L22 read, and L23 capture addresses on three already-open contexts and exactly matches uninstrumented eager controls. Its selector switches green→yellow, spoon→fork, and Madrid→Berlin. Restoring the baseline final-query routing row attenuates second-minus-first answer margins by 25.0%, 9.5%, and 40.0%, while all three changed-selector continuations remain selected over four tokens. ## 4. Diffusion: a scaffold with prompt-local programs and a latent register

Diffusion makes the proposed division of labor concrete: prompt-conditioned operands, a live binding operation, and a carried image register.

### 4.1 A fixed contextual source and a live read

The SDXL Q/K/V factorial supplies a native lowering for the dynamic address. At the measured binding unit, prompt conditioning produces `K_q,V_q`, the live latent produces `Q_t`, and native attention forms `C(q,z_t)=softmax(Q_t K_qᵀ / √d)`. In the call-zero factorial, live candidate Q with base K/V gave image-axis progress .72139; candidate K alone gave .66201; candidate V alone .71808; matched candidate K+V gave exact progress 1.000 and RGB MAE 0. The K×V interaction was .34130. This is causal conjunction under live Q, not independent mediation. For each prompt, K and V were byte-identical between calls 0 and 14. Q remained live-state dependent: candidate-versus-base Q cosine at call 14 was .83993 in block 0 and .82159 in block 1, compared with .99990 and .99012 at call 0. These are cross-prompt cosines at each call, not call-0-versus-call-14 temporal cosines. A prompt source can remain fixed at this boundary while the live query changes where and how it binds.

The timing result gives the scaffold a commitment interpretation. A base K/V lesion at call 0 left progress .72139; the same lesion only at call 14 left .99784. In this module, seed, rendering contrast, and call-zero cut, later native calls do not fully repair the early lesion's endpoint error. The first binding thus has persistent influence over the trajectory. Later writes remain capable of changing the register. The matched K/V pair is a contextual source program; Q is a live recipient and spatial term; the native writer and scheduler commit the bound result into the latent register.

### 4.2 Current operation over carried history

The scene-editing reproduction makes the distinction visible. In the blue-to-violet eye example, late installation at denoising calls 2+3 reaches violet irises while retaining more of the recipient composition; early installation at calls 0+1 moves portrait composition while leaving the eyes blue. The intervention copies the complete contextual target text stream at selected calls. Prompt row numbers describe those exact grammars and are not permanent semantic addresses. The target trajectory supplies the source, so the edit is target-assisted. It is a time-indexed source transport demonstration, not an iris-only module or donor-free compiler. In a separate terminating-boundary eye color × geometry assay, a 2×2 cross of target/recipient current transformer program with target/recipient incoming scheduler register gave eye-region projection .7124 for target program alone, .4035 for target register alone, and exact native-joint RGB when both target factors were installed. These projections use the native recipient-to-joint endpoint axis. This establishes joint endpoint authority of two operands, rather than independent mediation. See [EDIT].

### 4.3 Recurrence and scope boundaries

A cross-prompt grammar study found the same two-stage organization across four semantically admitted rows: anime-to-photographic rendering, mug-to-clock object identity, desert-to-snow scene structure, and red-to-blue eye color. Block 0 installed the dominant prompt-derived source, changed the live parent received by block 1, and made block 1 target-directed only on that updated parent. The mean block-0 gain on the base parent was .100834, the block-1 gain on the base parent was -.004334, the block-1 gain after candidate block 0 was .115666, and the interaction was .120000. The relation row was mechanically valid but not semantically admitted because the native candidate did not cleanly realize the requested relation. The shared grammar is therefore stronger than one concept-specific route, while the payload and semantic admission remain local. See [SDXL-GRAM]. This two-block factorial supports conditional refinement, rather than an independent mediator rescue.

The 2026-08-29 SDXL carrier trace first shows why one should not identify a semantic operation with one visible activation. Replacing a single complete state at a tested depth and time changed geometry and composition but left airplane identity, snow-scene identity, and blue eye color intact. Replacing the same route across the full 28-call interval flipped object and scene identity. Eye color survived the early route and was only recovered through the later up path. The same physical skeleton therefore carries factors with different temporal persistence. A failed singleton deletion is evidence for recurrent writing, redundancy, or re-derivation, not evidence that no carrier exists. See [SDXL-TIME].

The writer transport study adds a spatial warning. Unsigned main/skip envelopes recur with an early-main, middle/late-skip schedule, but signed payload orientation does not transfer through one factor-independent donor-to-seed map: the held mug-to-clock factor reverses which donor maps better, and wrong-time controls remain competitive. The surviving inference is a stable writer ABI with factor-, seed-, context-, and time-dependent transport, not a universal coordinate basis. See [WRITER].

E1's prospective text-address panel shows why dynamic support must include scope and payload. Frozen color and geometry supports produced positive whole-image progress on all three fresh color/geometry rows, with dose and sign sensitivity and near-zero row rolls. The unseen relation row missed the predicted lexical support at -.045 progress. A post-hoc whole-active-text transplant reached .882 but its nonlexical complement also moved .725 and duplicated the glass; the row-permuted control was .054. The positive whole-text result is therefore a broader state intervention with collateral, not a minimal relation circuit. The scaffold recurs as a route and writer organization while the semantic support can be larger, differently addressed, or not instrumented at the selected lexical grain. See [E1].

## 6. A mechanism statement: response-specific support on reusable interfaces

The records extend the first paper's circuit class into an architecture hierarchy: ordered execution forms causal state; recurring family-local interfaces assign source, read, writer, state, and consumer responsibilities; the current context selects the response-specific support on those interfaces. Qwen directly tests formation, predictive reader recurrence, selective consumption, and one bounded two-update path. Diffusion and Mamba implement subsets of the same anatomy, and the prospective comparison identifies only the common state-to-consumer edge. Reuse across executions and temporal feedback remain distinct claims.

The dynamic circuit is the response-specific causal support engaged for the current context and history, measured at a declared intervention grain. It can be distributed, redundant, or represented at different grains. Qwen's queried fact span may matter much more than the same patch under the other question. These examples establish selective participation, not a neuron-count, low-rank, or minimal-subgraph claim. The present evidence establishes coexistence of reusable interfaces, temporally formed state, and context-dependent support; it does not yet yield an exhaustive spatial/temporal taxonomy of dynamic circuits.

The central discriminator is selective reuse. The discovery-frozen reader profile predicts held relation contexts beyond three registered alternatives, while color, identity, and size show where operation-specific transfer or the readout fails. The same-parent query assay rejects the narrower prediction that identical stored bytes have query-blind relevance. Conventional lookup, contextual copying, redundancy, generic depth structure, and parallel paths remain compatible implementations within those bounds.

## 7. What the evidence rules out, and what it does not

The Qwen same-patch result establishes different causal participation of the same stored operand under different questions. It does not identify the query-to-source algorithm or exclude a conventional lookup implementation. The assay supplies exact source addresses, and literal value-token copying or redundant distributed storage could explain part of a fact-span transfer. The positive result is causal consumption under a changed query; autonomous semantic routing remains a separate mapping question.

The SDXL QKV result rules against a static downstream spatial address. Static K/V products did not make the attention map static because Q changed with the latent trajectory. It does not imply that every prompt product is static across architectures: the result is source-static within one SDXL execution boundary. Likewise, the FLUX eye edit rules against an isolated “eye-color wire” as the interpretation of a whole text-stream transplant. It supports bounded temporal appearance control with collateral and target assistance.

The carrier trace rules against using a single failed deletion as a null for a dynamic circuit. One state replacement can be repaired by later computation or bypassed by redundant carriage, while a repeated temporal clamp can defeat reconstitution. The E2 KV-cut countertrend and the weak color continuation rescue make the same point in autoregression: a late store can be a causal carrier without being the sole or uniquely necessary semantic route.

The corrected routing follow-up shows that one late row does not exhaust the selector's effect: restoring baseline routing leaves substantial answer-margin change and the changed-selector continuation. The older E6 layer-0 baseline belongs to the unqualified predecessor instrument and is not used as native causal evidence. E1's whole-text relation repair rules against confusing broad source scope with a minimal lexical circuit. E3's native-incompetent counting and later-query cells rule against turning a mechanically valid state shift into a semantic success. These are not discarded failures; they identify where a role claim ends.

The new feedback result does not rehabilitate E6/E8: it uses a different runtime and authority path. Its whole-row interventions show semantic authority of newly formed state, while their all-layer extent prevents attribution to L27 or to one microscopic writer. Its development case and one competent held case establish existence, not a broad domain frequency. The connected C6 forecast's modest loss and exact additive tie limit graph-level prediction, while its route/successor interventions preserve the signed causal path. Host-supplied queries, donors, and branch choices also leave autonomous addressing and autonomous iteration untested.

Final-weight disorder and developmental Pythia answer different questions. Qwen disorder demonstrates dependence of the current signed profile on two selected value-projection correspondences, with competence loss and no acquisition timeline. Pythia supplies a separate checkpoint trend, but one fixed random site wins a final row. Original pretraining onset remains unavailable. The evidence supports a recurring interface class without assuming a universal head, edge, route, writer, tensor address, or native symbol table.

## 9. Relation to existing circuit and binding accounts

Context-dependent binding and compact causal carriers have direct predecessors. Feng and Steinhardt identify binding-ID representations through causal interventions on contextual entities and attributes ([How do Language Models Bind Entities in Context?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17191)). Our same-parent assay supplies a complementary question: how does a fixed native K/V intervention acquire different authority under a changed query? It does not replace their representational account or establish a new binding algorithm.

Todd et al. identify compact function vectors carried by attention heads and demonstrate causal task effects across contexts ([Function Vectors in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15213)). Those results already show that a reusable operation can be represented in model state. The scaffold hypothesis concerns the recurring organization of construction, live addressing, writing, and carried state around particular operations; it does not presume that every dynamic circuit must be a compact vector.

Geva et al.'s staged recall account separates subject enrichment, relation propagation, and attention-mediated extraction ([Dissecting Recall of Factual Associations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14767)). The companion time paper tests construction and stored support at isolated K/V cuts. This bridge extends the question to recurring interfaces across operations, query-selective participation under identical interventions, and family-local source/program/register contrasts. Its contribution is the measured decomposition and the explicit scaffold hypothesis; universal role invariance and a complete feedback graph are subsequent empirical claims.

Geiger et al.'s [Causal Abstraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04709) supplies the theoretical setting for evaluating whether higher-level roles preserve a system's intervention behavior. The proposed role graph is a candidate abstraction with incomplete empirical alignment. [Circuit Tracing](https://www.transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/methods.html) already distinguishes prompt-specific interactions from context-independent global weights. A scaffold claim must add predictive causal reuse across operations and states beyond the observation that a prompt activates a particular graph. The current native-state assays supply pieces of that test, while the complete learned organization remains a working inference.

## 10. Conclusion

C7 now exercises a coarse connected conditional-formation graph across three native families, and C8 shows that selected native updates change formed-state authority under original consumers. Retained-history paths, composite diffusion reads, unequal optimizer doses, neighboring-layer gains and Mamba wrong-label transfer remain part of the result. A full microscopic alignment and original pretraining birth chronology remain stronger claims.

The inherited space–time circuit class describes how ordered execution forms state with later causal authority. The architecture result here is that this state inhabits reusable interfaces whose detailed semantic support remains context dependent. In Qwen, one prior-fixed late K/V interface carries several contents; the current question changes the authority of identical stored bytes; and a frozen microscopic relation profile predicts unopened contexts beyond three registered alternatives. The connected Qwen lane then joins a route, recipient-formed successor, and later score on three fresh contexts, with effects `+.011297`, `+.000376`, and `+.009138`. Its forecast is slightly worse than operation-blind (`.015099` versus `.014593` MAE), wins only 1/3 rows, and ties an additive rival, so that forecast result limits stronger graph discrimination while preserving the causal path. The material parent selects spoon rather than registered shoe; free steel follows that actual choice, while fixed leather is rank 2 behind selected. The color countertrend and identity/size readout failures bound each result to the operations the instrument can actually resolve.

The organization is tied to learned parameters at the final checkpoint: mild disorder of two selected value projections mostly preserves the signed profile, whereas complete row-and-bias permutation sharply changes it and degrades competence. This does not reveal when the organization was acquired. The stronger temporal result occurs within inference: native semantic predictions become newly formed all-layer state, and in development plus one competent held city/country case, changing only the second formed row changes the later answer while the first state and visible intermediate token remain fixed. The connected Mamba lane adds a second native path, L0 source → L18–20 S1 → distributed L18–23 S2 → later continuation, on two fresh held contexts; its post-held companion (`n_new=0`) closes complete S2 mediation for identity's original 13-token suffix and for a sealed ` located` capital diagnostic. These paths are host-orchestrated, target-assisted, non-unique, and terminally unassessed, but they establish more than passive cache persistence.

Diffusion and Mamba show why the scaffold should be stated in functional roles rather than shared tensors. Their sources, clocks, and state ABIs differ, yet prospectively aligned block, rescue, wrong-coordinate, and intact-parent assays support a common formed-state-to-native-consumer edge.
