# ISA model training and execution comparison

On three fresh seeds, all models answered every isolated test correctly. FieldLM and the native SPF adapter preserved every continuous intermediate state. The neural selector was faster at inference but made one routing error that propagated for eleven steps before later instructions restored the correct final state. These observations describe the current candidate-selection adapters on eleven known x86 byte-ALU forms.

## Accuracy and execution cost

The revised campaign used seeds 101, 137 and 173. Every model received the same 352 discovery observations per seed, with separate feedback and heldout panels. Heldout evidence totals 1,056 isolated steps, 384 continuous steps and twelve final-state checks per model. All execution used Beast's CPU, one Torch/BLAS thread and scalar inference. There was no GPU use.

## What the learner acquired

All three learn which supplied transition operator applies. Exact candidate code computes the arithmetic and flags. FieldLM/SPF use supplied instruction-key separation and exact addressed recall; the NN embeds the raw two-byte window and before-state features. This gives the addressed models an explicit operand-invariance prior, while the NN must learn to ignore irrelevant immediate/state features for routing. The SPF adapter is a native phase/read component, not a full contextual SPFTransformer.

In seed 101, the first neural divergence was heldout trace 3, step 13: bytes `24fa` mean AND AL, 250, but the learned head selected DEC. Its DEC logit was 5.522 versus AND's 5.147. That wrong transition affected eleven successive state comparisons; later execution recovered, so the final-state score alone hid the error. Seeds 137 and 173 had exact continuous execution. Discovery cross-entropy fell from approximately 2.42 to 0.006 in all three specimens. This is a positive training trajectory alongside a retained generalization failure.

The scope is operand/state and sequence generalization within the supplied instruction forms. It does not establish learned arithmetic, unseen instruction-family discovery, full x86 coverage, architectural priority or a speed ranking for full FieldLM/SPFTransformer implementations. Terminal certification was not run.

## Original campaign and controller correction

The original campaign on seeds 17, 29 and 43 achieved 100% isolated accuracy for all models, but NN continuous accuracy was 369/384 (96.09%) and final-state accuracy was 11/12. Its controller compared only discrete feedback scores. When those scores saturated, it rejected lower-loss futures as equivalent; accepted NN updates were 64, 64 and 96 out of 128 physical updates. Two rejected futures for a seed repeated the same deterministic candidate. All original candidate artifacts and receipts are preserved.

The revised continuation policy adds discovery-only cross-entropy improvement while retaining non-regression on feedback isolated, continuous and endpoint scores. It accepted all 128 updates on fresh seeds. This was an instrument/controller correction motivated by saturated feedback and observed training progress. The panels have different seeds, so their accuracy difference is not a matched estimate of the policy's causal benefit. The new intermediate error remains visible.
