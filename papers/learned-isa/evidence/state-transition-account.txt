native CPU FP32/thread1, raw instruction `4883c701`, register R7=508..511,
other registers/flags zero. These are repeat captures of the existing native
source square, not new independent semantic examples. The model receives raw
instruction/register/flag bits. Supplied ADD1 evaluates predictions; no oracle
after-state, semantic label, training update or repair enters a forward.

M1 is mixer1's residual output and post1's input. P1 is post1's write. X2=M1+P1
feeds the final mixer. Its decay D, local scan writer w and completed state H
remain distinct from its projected residual write W2. M2=X2+W2 feeds post2,

Completion is always actual FP32 `(10+01)-00`. Full tensors are completed.
Native clamps are supplied intervention state, explicitly recorded in typed
receipts. One immutable prepared parent seals all candidate payloads before
suffix execution; no physical batch fusion is claimed.
