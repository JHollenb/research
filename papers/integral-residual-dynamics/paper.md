# Integral Residual Dynamics: Models Consume Trajectory Functionals, Not Sites

**A theory-and-certificate companion to the distributed-store measurements, with the four-quadrant certificate definitions, the cross-family sink grid, the scale floor, the transport-cut result, and the consumption kernel**

Jacob Hollenbeck

*2026-08-21; revised 2026-09-30*

> Every number in this paper is measured. Where a receipt is bundled in a companion paper in this repository it is cited by path; where it lives only in the private experiment store it is cited by job ID and marked as such.

---

## Abstract

A single-site causal test asks whether the state at one layer, head, position or denoising step is necessary or sufficient for a behavior. When that test returns a null at every site, the usual reading is "no localized mechanism here." We argue for a different reading in a specific, measured regime: the object the model's output-reader consumes is not the residual at any one site, $r(t^\ast)$, but a functional of the residual trajectory, $R = \mathcal{I}(r(t))$, accumulated across depth or denoising time. We call this **Integral Residual Dynamics (IRD)**. The claim is falsifiable by a four-quadrant certificate: a mechanism is *path-constituted* only when single-site ablation and single-site writing both fail while whole-path ablation removes the behavior and whole-path writing installs it. That exact cross is not what a localized circuit produces, and not what a diffuse-but-linear representation produces; only a path-constituted store produces it. This paper is the theory and certificate-definition companion to two measurement papers in this repository: the distributed-store methods paper [1], which contains the primary numbers and their offline verifiers, and the certified diffusion-circuit paper [2], which supplies the four-quadrant precedent. Here we (i) define the object and its eight operational certificates C1–C8; (ii) report the cross-family sink grid — the four-quadrant signature issued in 8 of 8 measured cells across one diffusion model and three autoregressive families at or below 8B parameters, with a 30B mixture-of-experts identity extension; (iii) give the scale floor (a capable 70M six-layer model concentrates 79–81% of the whole-path necessity in one layer; the distributed signature appears by roughly 400M); (iv) report the transport-cut result (a state-space model does not certify at a residual-stream cut, but two transformers *lose their own certificates at that same cut*, so the certificate speaks about a cut, not an architecture); and (v) report the consumption kernel on the diffusion time axis, front-loaded and non-exchangeable, together with the 2026-09-22 correction that fixed what that kernel measures. We keep the theory's own falsifiers in view: a universal segment-level integration law failed, destructive necessity weights were poor constructive editors at 1.7B, a hallucination predictor was struck under adversarial review, and every grid cell has n = 4 or fewer. The surviving claim is bounded: within the tested envelope the output-committing object is a path-constituted integral of the formed residual, certifiable by the four-quadrant asymmetry.

## 1. What the null means

Causal-intervention interpretability localizes a behavior by intervening at one site and reading the change in output [3, 4, 5, 6]. A null at every site — no single layer, head, position or step whose ablation hurts or whose insertion helps — is routinely read as evidence that the behavior has no localized mechanism at the probed granularity. That reading is correct when the relevant information is concentrated. It is a category error when the output reads from a store that is written redundantly across many sites, because then single-site ablation is compensated by the rest of the store and single-site insertion is too small to matter. Self-repair in language models [7, 8], and the timing-lock structure of iterative generators, are known routes to exactly this pattern; best-practice guides already warn that patching granularity changes conclusions [5, 6].

The methods paper [1] shows measured cases where the wrong reading is available: in a production diffusion transformer, ablating any one of four route components at any one denoising step leaves at least 22.2% of an identity transition intact, while ablating the same four at every step leaves 0.13% and inserting them across all steps carries 91.1%, on held-out seeds; in several language models, replacing a subject token's cached keys and values at one layer removes a small fraction of the effect while replacing them across the second half of the layers removes nearly all of it and flips the answer. Those are the observations. This paper is about the object they imply and the certificate that decides when the implication is licensed.

A note on one number that became folklore. An earlier internal note cited a "0.2218 / 0.0013" single-step / all-step pair for the diffusion identity contrast. A same-day instrument audit (the campaign's Addendum K) found that the pair as cited was not in the record and replaced it with the actual step-accumulated mediation panel (receipt in private experiment store, job-7d53e47755f7). The receipt-backed statement is the one used throughout: single-step ablation leaves **≥ 0.222** of the transition and all-step ablation leaves **0.0013** ([1], §4.1, §4.6; bundled receipt `job-6650205b45eb`). We use only receipt-backed numbers, and we flag the folklore because it is a good example of the failure mode this paper is about — a plausible pair of numbers that no instrument actually produced.

## 2. The object

Split the state at any typed internal cut into a prompt-invariant *scaffold* plus a prompt-specific *residual*:

> `x(t) = s(t) + r(t)`

where `t` indexes the accumulation axis (transformer depth, or denoising step). The scaffold `s(t)` is the positional and template structure shared across inputs; the residual `r(t)` is the part that carries the semantic distinction under test. The **wiring fact** — that the output-reader is a linear read of the last residual, so anything controlling the output is present there when the reader runs — is close to a tautology and is not the claim.

The **IRD claim** is about which function of the residual the reader consumes. Single-step necessity tests `r(t*)` at one site and, in the path-constituted regime, reports "no circuit." The consumer tests the trajectory functional

> `R = \mathcal{I}(r(t))`.

Pointwise instruments are not weak in this regime; they are aimed at the wrong integral. The **sink corollary** follows: IRD is not one more circuit alongside the others. It is a *sink* — site-local circuits write into a late, path-constituted accumulation, and that accumulation is what commits the output.

Two things keep this from being a restatement of "the residual stream is a bus." First, the certificate below is a *shape* the data did not have to have: a localized circuit passes at least one single-site quadrant, and a diffuse-but-linear representation fails the whole-path quadrants too. Second, §7 shows the certificate is a statement about a *cut*, not about the residual stream in general — move the cut and the same model's certificate can appear or vanish, which a pure "everything is on the bus" account does not predict.

## 3. The four-quadrant certificate

For a behavior with a clean source and target condition, and a declared model, cut, address, intervention and unchanged native consumer, run four interventions and judge each by the unchanged rest of the model, run to completion and read out through its own output head (for a language model, the next-token distribution over a frozen distractor set; for the diffusion model, the unchanged suffix, scheduler and decoder to pixels):

|  | single site | whole path |
|---|---|---|
| **necessity** (ablate) | behavior survives | behavior dies |
| **sufficiency** (write) | nothing happens | full authorship |

"Whole path" means the same component at every denoising step (diffusion) or the same token position's cached keys and values across a contiguous range of layers (language models). A cell is **path-certified** only when all four quadrants pass: every registered single-site necessity share stays below the concentration bar, whole-path ablation reaches the declared task floor, every registered single-site write stays below the sufficiency bar, and the whole-path write reaches the native-consumer threshold. Exact no-op (a zero-magnitude write must reproduce the native output bit-for-bit) and exact uninstall are hard validity gates, not successes; so are clean capability and generic-floor separation (§4, C2–C3).

Three reading rules govern the certificate, carried over from the methods paper [1] and made explicit here.

1. **The cut matters.** Replacing a subject token's *residual stream* at one early layer removes the whole effect in every language model tested, because the residual stream carries everything downstream of it. The single-site nulls that make the certificate meaningful are per-layer key/value slices and per-step diffusion components, not the residual stream. §7 turns this observation into a result.
2. **How the key/value swap is done matters.** An *in-forward* swap, applied inside one forward pass, lets the subject token's own attention read the replaced entries, which changes the subject's residual from that layer onward and silently turns a single-layer swap into a multi-layer one. The *isolated* swap runs the subject token normally, replaces the cached entries, then runs only later tokens, so only later reads change. The two can disagree completely ([1], §4.3, reports a single-layer in-forward effect of 0.99 against an isolated 0.00 on the same model). The isolated swap is the primary measurement.
3. **Whole-path ablation is close to deleting the token.** Replacing a token's keys and values at every layer is nearly the same as removing it from context, so the informative result is the single-site *failure* and the size of the gap, not the whole-path success by itself.

## 4. The certificate suite (C1–C8)

Each certificate is an operational, falsifiable measurement: fp32, no-op-gated, with a norm-matched sham null on every positive and the same audit effort spent on every refutation as on every pass. The suite is the standing reference the campaign froze; designs and receipts are in the private experiment store, and the load-bearing numbers below are the ones bundled in the companion papers [1, 2] where available.

- **C1 — Sink certificate (four-quadrant, at a transport cut).** The output-committing object is a trajectory functional of carrier residual traces, measured as the four-quadrant table at the key/value cut. *Status:* issued from the step where capability arrives in a 24-layer 410M model and sharpens thereafter; scale floor below (§6). This is the certificate the grid in §5 reports.
- **C2 — Capability certificate (strict gate).** A carrier-swap log-probability movement (`pc_shift`) plus the strict gate `capable_strict = clean-capable AND NOT floor-capable`. The floor arm catches the scene-prior confound, a model answering from prior without the carrier. *Status:* load-bearing at 8B and 30B, where identity and binding are floor-capable (the scene alone elicits the answer) and naive cells are invalid; a state-space model was 8 of 10 strict with zero floor-capable cells (§7).
- **C3 — Kernel-cell validity gate.** Before any kernel fit or normalized-progress claim: `(clean − floor) ≥ 1 nat` AND `all-arm Δlp ≤ −1 nat`, else the cell is invalid because the scene-prior floor makes the normalizer meaningless. *Status:* the 8B identity cell is invalid (gap 0.13 nats); the 8B arithmetic cell is valid (gap 7.40 nats). The gate is re-checked per (family, object) cell.
- **C4 — Depth/time kernel.** A segment-dose response fit (linear vs relaxation vs saturating) over schedules × segments; the portable summaries are the first-half/second-half mass ratio and the sign pattern. *Status:* non-linear beats linear in 5 of 5 valid cells; kernels are family-tuned (§8). "Universal integration law" is retired.
- **C5 — Attribution certificate.** Per-carrier ablation at the sink: carriers must remove ≥ 20% of the joint effect and the norm-matched half-rolled sham must remove ≤ 10%. *Status:* non-monotonic in scale — passes at ≤ 1.7B, fails at 8B, passes at 30B — with the standing caveat that a sham *at carrier positions* destroys a large share (45% at 30B), so attribution here is position-specific, not direction-specific. Issue C5 only where C2-strict holds.
- **C6 — Redundancy ratio.** A per-layer necessity profile with a validity gate; the separator is the ratio of single-layer recoveries to the joint effect. *Status:* recall behaviors sit below 1 (redundant, compensating), compute behaviors at or above 1 (decomposable, superadditive); replicates at 1.7B and 8B; unrun at 30B.
- **C7 — Externalization certificate.** A chain-of-thought scratchpad-token key/value swap against an input-carrier-only swap on a task where the plain form is incapable and the scratchpad form is capable: if the scratchpad swap dominates, the scratchpad tokens are load-bearing state, not commentary. *Status:* passes in four families — the committed answer flips with log-probability shifts 8.02 (1.7B), 9.23 (0.5B), 14.18 (8B) and 11.59 (30B MoE) — the single strongest result in the program.
- **C8 — Edit-transfer.** Whether ablation-derived kernel weights transfer to constructive edits. *Status:* family-dependent. At 1.7B the kernel-weighted write is indistinguishable from sham (refuted there); at 8B it recovers 64–83% of single-site authorship at a third to a half of its collateral bleed (best gen-per-bleed on the panel), with weights that had to cross objects (from the arithmetic cell) to get there. Destructive necessity weights are not constructive write weights.

Struck or refuted probes, retained in the ledger and not re-issued without redesign: a hallucination predictor (label deviation from the frozen rule plus a set-composition artifact; the ablation-only component was at chance); a damage≈necessity ordering (refuted in four families); a saturation/early-exit equivalence (a self-comparison by construction).

## 5. The cross-family sink grid

The four-quadrant signature was issued in **8 of 8** measured cells spanning one diffusion model and three autoregressive families at or below 8B, across three behavior classes (identity, color, verb), plus a computed arithmetic object and a 30B mixture-of-experts extension. The grid is a grid of *named cells*, not eight interchangeable replications of one task.

| cell (family, behavior) | single-site necessity | whole-path necessity | whole-path sufficiency | receipt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| diffusion, identity | ≥ 0.222 of transition survives every site×step | 0.0013 remains (all steps) | 0.911 carried | [1] §4.1; bundled `job-6650205b45eb` |
| 1.7B, identity | L12 +0.031, L18 +0.403 nats (≤ 19.3% of path) | all-layer −2.093 nats → answer flips | full K/V trace writes ≈ 1.06–1.09 | bundled `job-6650205b45eb` (necessity); private `job-0a07cb92d360` (write) |
| 1.7B, color | single-layer below bar | second half removes effect, flips answer | full trace writes | bundled `job-72f0e98679d5` |
| 1.7B, verb | single-layer below bar | second half removes effect | "swimming" writes (full pass) | private `job-9c6453d29ac1`; grid `job-72f0e98679d5` |
| 0.5B, identity | single-layer below bar | second half removes effect, flips | full trace writes | bundled `job-72f0e98679d5` |
| 0.5B, color | single-layer below bar | second half removes effect, flips | full trace writes | bundled `job-72f0e98679d5` |
| 8B, identity | single-layer below bar | all-arm −2.451 nats (fp32) | full trace writes | bundled `job-6299f2391d5c` |
| 8B, color | single-layer below bar | all-arm removes effect (gap 7.40 nats valid) | full trace writes | grid `job-72f0e98679d5` |

The whole-path necessity for the 1.7B identity cell is a clean example of the shape: the clean prompt answers *fox* at a +2.958-nat margin over its top distractor; ablating layer 12's subject key/value moves the answer log-probability by +0.031 nat and layer 18's by +0.403 nat (1.5% and 19.3% of the whole-path effect), the first half of the stack by +0.357 nat, and the whole stack by −2.093 nats, flipping the answer to *wolf* (bundled `job-6650205b45eb`). The store is the second half of the stack, distributed, with no single late layer necessary or sufficient.

**Sufficiency and coordinate interchange.** The write half of C1 was established by a cross-slot interchange (private experiment store, `job-0a07cb92d360`): the same symbol displacement written into a 1.7B model's hidden slot (W1) and into its full key/value trace (W2) produces nearly identical consumer scores — W1 hidden 0.90–1.02 against W2 full-trace 1.058–1.093 — while a single-layer key/value slice carries almost nothing (0.010–0.107) and shams stay ≤ 0.047; the difference `|s(W1) − s(W2_all)|` is 0.04–0.12, inside the frozen 0.25 bar. One residual, two coordinate systems, distributed across depth. (Disclosure carried forward: that job was killed by a VRAM leak after the interchange leg completed; its numbers come from the leg's log receipt, and the fix is recorded beside the frozen design.) The object also crosses the model boundary: a native diffusion displacement pushed backward through an anchored bridge into a foreign 1.7B model's feature space, written into the hidden slot and judged by that model's own head, reaches ceiling parity with the model's own displacement — bridged 0.9916 / 1.0029 / 0.9531 against true 0.9915 / 1.0047 / 0.9533 for three targets, shams −0.165 / −0.001 / +0.103, no-op exactly 0.0 (bundled `job-69e2f6a6b492`).

**The instrument correction inside the grid.** One cell did not close on the first attempt. A promotion pass to 8B identity appeared to *survive* whole-path ablation — necessity seemed to fail — until a one-line readout (asking the model what it answers when the scene never names a subject) showed the 8B model already says *fox* at a +1.438-nat margin from the scene alone. "Survives ablation" was "the scene already says so." The necessity bar was amended before any rerun to require the ablated output to land *at* the generic-prompt floor, and a multi-filler floor replaced the single most-biased filler. The last cell then missed the amended bar by 0.014 nat, recorded verbatim; read-only forensics found that every value sat on a 1/16-nat grid — the bf16 logit unit in the last place — so a clause comparing log-probabilities across distributions could not be decided at bf16. A preregistered fp32 disambiguator, run on two independent backends, passed the amended bar (`lp_gap` 0.267 against a 0.5 bar), confirmed the floor bias directly (three fillers at +1.472 / +0.536 / +0.030 nats), and the two backends agreed to 0.0001 nat on every graded quantity (bundled `job-6299f2391d5c`). The grid closed 8 of 8 only after the confound was measured and the instrument fixed; that sequence is part of the result, not a footnote to it.

**30B extension.** The identity certificate extends to a 30B mixture-of-experts model: `pc_shift` 22.6, single-site necessity ≤ 2.9% of the all-path effect, whole-trace write ≈ 1.04, path necessity inside the multi-filler floor band at 0.062 nat (receipts in private experiment store, `job-38c1c37dd591`, `job-7cc5b24954f0`, `job-ef8408d9c3d2`). The arithmetic object at 30B is **issued-qualified, not certified**: whole-path ablation destroys the computed answer but overshoots 2.09 nats below the floor, failing the two-sided gap clause in the destroying direction (recovery 118.9% against a certified band of 92–116%). We keep that distinction rather than pooling both rows as "30B closure." The extension also exposed a systems hazard worth its own line: a paged loader silently zero-filled 58 GB of fused expert tensors it could not find, and only an out-of-memory kill prevented a full gate-passing quadrant table from a model whose entire expert bank was zeros. Raise-on-miss materialization is now a hard custody gate.

## 6. The scale floor

The certificate has a developmental floor, and this is a positive result about where the topology changes, not only a limit. In a 24-layer 410M model, the identity certificate arrives *with* capability — it is absent at a pre-capability checkpoint and present from the checkpoint where the task is first solved — and sharpens afterward (the best single-layer write moves from 0.257 to −0.025 across training). In a 6-layer 70M model, capable checkpoints carry **79–81%** of the whole-path necessity in one layer: concentrated, not distributed. The path-constituted signature therefore appears somewhere between 6 and 24 layers; in the methods paper's language-model panel it is present by roughly 400M and absent in a 124M model, where 20 sparse-autoencoder features at one layer remove the whole effect ([1], §6). A capable small model is a genuine counterexample to a scale-free distributed sink, and a pre-capability checkpoint is inert to every intervention — the certificate fires on the presence of the mechanism, not on the mere ability to run the task. (Receipts for the training-checkpoint sweep are in the private experiment store, V1 battery.)

## 7. The certificate speaks about a cut, not an architecture

A state-space (Mamba-family) model does not certify at a residual-stream (hidden-state) cut. The tempting reading is "this architecture lacks the mechanism." The decisive control refutes that reading: two transformers that *do* certify at their key/value cut **lose their own certificates at the same hidden-state cut** — single-site necessity moves from 1.0–1.5% of the path effect to 172–236% when only the cut changes, nothing else. So the hidden-state cut is the wrong transport interface for this measurement in every architecture, and the certificate is a statement about a *cut*, not about the residual stream in general or about an architecture. The residual stream is a bus; the certificate needs the cut that carries the payload, which for transformers is the key/value cache and for a state-space model is an as-yet-unimplemented recurrent-state cut. The state-space model's path-half does hold (carrier-swap shifts 12.41 / 2.26, a floor-and-flip, a flat depth profile, early commitment), and its capability floors are unusually clean (8 of 10 strict, zero floor-capable), but no architecture comparison is licensed until the recurrent-state cut passes a parity gate. (Receipts in private experiment store; arch/scale jobs `job-13a15543fbb1`, `job-8e2d02c4a51e`, `job-0622676c3473`, `job-f11cab813f66`.)

This is the sharpest reason the theory is not "the residual stream is a bus" restated: on a bus account, a single early residual cut would be *maximally* necessary, which is exactly what we measure (172–236%), and no four-quadrant cross would exist anywhere. The cross exists at the key/value cut and vanishes at the residual cut in the *same* model. The object is real; its address is a transport interface.

## 8. The consumption kernel, and what it measures

If the object is an integral, the integral has a measure. On the diffusion consumption axis, writing the target displacement at different subsets of the four denoising steps and scoring reproduction of the target render gives a front-loaded, decaying weight: for one representative cell the per-step contributions are ≈ [0.21, 0.22, 0.08, 0.04], and the all-step write scores 0.73 while step 0 alone scores 0.13 and a late double-dose at equal nominal integral scores only 0.16 (bundled `job-6650205b45eb`; the cross-family kernels span ≈ [0.21–0.43, 0.18–0.25, 0.04–0.13, 0.01–0.06]). Dose is not exchangeable across time: doubling the late steps at equal nominal integral reaches 0.055–0.288 against the all-step arm's 0.522–0.810, and a path-dependence test at identical net-zero dose moves the output by +0.27…+0.51 in one order and −0.08…−0.49 in the reverse (private grid `job-72f0e98679d5`). You cannot buy back early steps late; the functional is not a plain sum over time.

**The 2026-09-22 correction.** The kernel's headline figure was originally captioned as if it measured *identity change*. It does not. Progress here is `1 − pixel MAD` to the target render, so it measures *reproduction of the target image*, not whether the identity changed. Under that correction the step-0-only write (progress 0.13) renders a recognizable fox, and the late double-dose write (progress 0.16) renders a recognizable lion in a different pose and coat, which pixel distance scores poorly ([1], §4.6, and the dated correction in the demo record). Early steps fix layout; late steps can still change what the animal is. The kernel is a real, reproducible measure of image-reproduction dose response; it is not a measure of when the semantic content is decided, and the original caption confused the two. We keep the corrected reading.

**The universal-kernel falsifier.** A stronger claim — one segment-level integration law across families — failed its own battery. On the depth axis the kernel is non-linear (a saturating form beats the linear one in every valid held-out cell), but the *shape* is family- and object-tuned: a held-out 1.4B model keeps only a coarse second-half-loaded shape (second-half mass 0.31, saturating fit MSE 0.035 against a linear 2.24) and peaks at a different segment than the fit family; an 8B arithmetic kernel flips sign at one segment; a 30B kernel is bimodal with a mass ratio of 1.009, margin-fragile against the late-loaded clause. What generalizes is "second-half-loaded, saturating"; segment-level universality is retired, and the campaign's own earlier "compounding" story was demoted to second order by the theory's own out-of-sample selection rule. On the diffusion consumption axis a signed weighted-sum form won an out-of-sample selection (held-out MSE 0.0177 against 0.0202 and 0.0218 for a relaxation and a saturating variant); these are two different axes with two different winners, and we report both rather than forcing one law.

## 9. What survived the batteries, and what broke

Three batteries were run to break the theory, and parts of it broke; the surviving claims are the ones that survived that.

- **Extended (survived and strengthened).** A *computed* object — the answer " eight", which appears nowhere in "The sum of three and five equals" — certifies with the full four-quadrant signature: whole-carrier ablation −5.41 nats onto the task floor, single layers ≤ 1.5%, and the "two and seven" trace authoring " nine" at a write score of 0.979 (private `job-3b014f386fb8`). Freshly constructed objects integrate like recalled ones. Chain-of-thought externalization (C7) replicated across four families up to 30B.
- **Bounded.** The transport-cut result (§7): the certificate is about a cut. The scale floor (§6): below roughly 400M the store is concentrated.
- **Falsifiers fired, in writing.** A universal segment-level kernel (§8). Kernel-weighted constructive editing at 1.7B (C8; ≈ sham against best-single-site 0.94–1.02), family-dependent at 8B. A formation falsifier at 70M (the scale floor).
- **Struck under adversarial review.** A hallucination predictor was withdrawn entirely: the published label deviated from the frozen full-vocabulary rule, pooling created a set-composition artifact, the apparent signal was carried by the clean-confidence term, and the ablated top-1 did not become the claimed prior in any of 200 cells. This is a measurement-design negative control for any future paper, not evidence for the thesis. A saturation/early-exit equivalence and a depth-order redundancy reversal were also exposed as self-comparisons or sign-mixed noise and struck.

## 10. Limits

- **Breadth.** One lab; single-token readouts; four transformer families at or below 8B plus one 30B mixture-of-experts identity cell and one diffusion model; no external replication. Every grid cell has n = 4 or fewer, and multi-seed necessity for some cells is single-seed (sufficiency and mediation are multi-seed in the diffusion case [2]).
- **A path certificate identifies topology, not every computation.** Whole-path writes can be non-minimal; a certificate says the behavior is path-constituted at the tested cut, not that the path is the unique or minimal one.
- **Representation, read authority, and write policy are separate claims.** Successful anchored interchange (§5) does not imply zero-shot portability; a destructive necessity kernel is not an editing policy (§8, C8); consumer-relative interchange at named slots is not a universal object code.
- **Instrument sensitivity.** Generic-prompt floors, near-tied logits at bf16, and source-model capability make necessity particularly instrument-sensitive; the grid closed only after a floor confound was measured and a bf16 unit-in-the-last-place artifact was escalated to fp32 (§5).
- **Open.** A recurrent-state cut for state-space models; multi-token and reasoning-chain objects beyond the scratchpad result; the redundancy ratio and edit transfer at 30B (jobs cancelled by operator order); external replication.

## 11. Relation to the companion measurement papers

This paper does not duplicate the numbers it depends on. The methods paper [1] ("Single-Site Tests Miss Distributed Stores") is the empirical and methods paper: it contains the four-quadrant test as an instrument, the primary language-model and diffusion tables, the preregistered sparse-autoencoder comparison on public tools and models, the blind-graded comparison of four standard readouts, and the offline verifiers (`python3 verify.py` re-derives the sink and kernel headline values from the same receipts this paper cites). The certified diffusion-circuit paper [2] ("The Circuit That Survived Its Coordinates") supplies the four-quadrant precedent, the time-accumulation result (single-step interventions fail necessity and sufficiency at every site while all-step interventions pass both), the coordinate-freedom result, the function–substrate separation, and a single-file CPU verifier for a certified induction circuit in a public 70M model. IRD is the theory those two measure: the object is a path-constituted integral of the formed residual, the certificate is the four-quadrant cross, and the certificates C1–C8 are the operational tests. Where this paper reports a number that is bundled in [1] or [2], the bundle is the authority; where a number lives only in the private experiment store, it is cited by job ID and the derivation is not re-runnable from this repository.

## 12. Related work

Activation patching, path patching and causal tracing [3, 4, 9] are the instruments this framing reinterprets; Zhang and Nanda [5] and Heimersheim and Nanda [6] show conclusions depend on metric and granularity, which is the precondition for a single-site null to be a category error. The Hydra effect [7] and broader self-repair [8] are the compensation mechanism that makes a distributed store's single-site ablations null; IRD names the object those nulls are hiding and gives it a certificate. Sparse autoencoders [10] and attribution patching [11] are complementary decomposition tools; the methods paper's sparse-feature arm [1] shows a subject-restricted feature ablation across all layers recovers most of the effect once the ablation spans the path, which is the feature-level statement of the same object. The diffusion-circuit precedent and its traps are in [2]. The theory's contribution is the split `x = s + r`, the trajectory functional `R = \mathcal{I}(r(t))`, the four-quadrant certificate that distinguishes a path-constituted store from both a localized circuit and a diffuse-but-linear representation, and the transport-cut result that keeps the claim from collapsing into "the residual stream is a bus."

## 13. Conclusion

In a bounded but non-trivial envelope — four transformer families at or below 8B, a 30B mixture-of-experts identity cell, one diffusion model, recalled and computed single-token objects — the object a model's output-reader commits is not the residual at any one site or step but a functional of the residual trajectory accumulated across a transport cut. The four-quadrant certificate decides when that reading is licensed, and it issued in 8 of 8 measured cells while its stronger cousins (a universal kernel, a constructive editing recipe, a hallucination predictor) failed and were written down as failures. The claim is falsifiable, scale-floored, cut-specific, and single-lab; the certificate, and the discipline of spending as much audit effort on every pass as on every refutation, is what we hope others hold this against.
